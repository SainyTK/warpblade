WARPBLADE

Kael, a warp knight of the night ruins. He throws his crystal sword and appears wherever it lands, casts fire, ice and lightning, and grows stronger with every wave.

Skill tree

Paths run left to right. Skills joined by "or" exclude each other for the whole run. Fusion skills need one skill from each of two paths. Unspent points carry over.

Moves

A D
Walk. Tap a side twice and hold to run. Arrow keys work too.
W
Jump. Hold S and jump to drop through a ledge.
J
Sword combo: a light cut, then a heavy finisher. Combos grow to six hits as you level up. Each hit gives back a little MP.
W then J
Air attack. In the air, the last hit spikes the foe into the floor.
L
Dash. Nothing can touch Kael while he dashes, and it cancels an attack. Dash through an attack just as it lands for a perfect dodge: the world slows down and Kael gets MP back.
K
Warp strike. Kael throws his sword at the nearest foe in front and appears where it lands, hitting harder the farther he travels.
U
Special. Kael starts with Crystal Nova; the skill tree can replace it with a stronger ultimate. Hold to charge up to four levels, release to strike. Level 4 spends a full MP bar.

Spells and damage

I
Cast the selected spell (18 MP).
O
Switch element.
Fire
A flask of flame that bursts on contact and leaves the ground burning.
Ice
A line of crystal spikes runs along the floor and freezes what it touches.
Bolt
Lightning strikes the nearest foe in front and arcs to one more.
BACK
Hits from behind deal 1.5 times the damage. Foes take a moment to turn around.
AIR
Foes knocked into the air take double damage until they land. Each hit holds them up a little less.
BLOCK
From wave 3, husks block sword hits from the front while they walk, and strike back after two blocks. Hit them from behind, while they attack, or with a heavy finisher. Wisps slip away from swings; catch them after they lunge. A swing that hits nothing leaves Kael open for longer.

Stage

Thirty waves. A Rift Brute comes every fifth wave and the Rift Warden at waves 20 and 30. After each wave the skill tree opens: spend your points or keep them for later. Kael gains a skill point every level.
