WARPBLADE

Kael, a warp knight of the night ruins. He throws his crystal sword and appears wherever it lands, and he casts fire, ice and lightning.

Moves

A D
Run. Arrow keys work too.
W / Space
Jump, press again in the air for a second jump. Hold S and jump to drop through a ledge.
J
Sword combo. Tap up to three times: cross cut, rising cut, falling smash. Each hit gives back a little MP.
K
Warp strike (10 MP). Kael throws his sword at the nearest foe in front and appears where it lands, hitting harder the farther he travels. Hold W to warp to a floating crystal and hang there to recover MP quickly.
Shift
Phase dodge. Kael can't be hit while he dashes.

Spells

L
Cast the selected spell (18 MP).
Q
Switch element.
Fire
A flask of flame that bursts on contact and leaves the ground burning.
Ice
A line of crystal spikes runs along the floor and freezes what it touches.
Bolt
Lightning strikes the nearest foe in front and arcs to one more.
