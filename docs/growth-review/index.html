Why Warpblade stays flat

  1. Most growth is added, not multiplied. Kael starts with 10 attack damage and gains 0.6 per level; Keen Edge adds 2 per rank and the shop adds 1 per level. Only Swift Hands (+8% speed per rank), Berserker (+25%) and Archmage (+20% ability power) multiply, and nothing multiplies those again. (src/constants/characters/kael.ts, src/utils/stats.ts)
  2. Enemies grow in a straight line too: +9% HP per wave, so wave 30 foes have 3.6 times the HP, and at most 9 foes are on screen. (WAVE_SCALE and MAX_FOES in src/constants/waves.ts)
  3. Because both sides grow at about the same slow rate, killing a typical foe takes about one second at wave 1 and about one second at wave 29. The fight never changes shape.
  4. A run has about 17 decisions (one per level, level 19 at wave 30). Vampire Survivors gives about 80 level-ups plus a chest about every 2 minutes.
  5. Most cards add a new move or button. A new move changes how Kael plays but adds little damage, and the strongest cards are the same every run: in runs that always take the strongest card, Lightning, Ice Spikes, Fire Flask, Swift Sigils, Crystal Nova and Keen Edge are in 90% or more of the final builds.
  6. Nothing combines. Vampire Survivors weapons gain from shared passives (Might, Cooldown, Amount, Area, Duration) and each weapon evolves with one partner passive. Warpblade has three spell evolutions, each worth one spell, and no card makes another card stronger.
  7. The run ends without showing what dealt the damage, so a player cannot tell which choice won or lost the run.

Direction to agree on

  • Growth on the scale of Vampire Survivors: a strong build ends hundreds of times stronger than it started, a weak one tens of times. The gap between them, together with the player's hands, decides the late waves.
  • Enemies grow about as fast as a median build, so a strong build pulls ahead and a weak one falls behind in the middle of the run. The growth comes from many more foes on screen than today's 9, as well as more HP per foe.
  • Damage comes from a small set of "sources" the hero carries (for example the sword string, a familiar, an orbiting blade, a spell that fires on its own, a trap). Each source levels up to a cap and has slots, so taking one means giving up another.
  • Shared multiplying stats (damage, speed, area, count, duration, crit) lift every source at once, and each source evolves with one specific partner, so the player builds toward a plan.
  • Far more decisions: a level up every 20 to 40 seconds early, chests from mini bosses, and reroll, skip and banish to fight bad luck.
  • An end of run screen with damage, damage per second and time held for every source, plus the level and kills.
  • Skill play stays: back, air and crit hits still multiply, so a good player gets more out of the same build.
