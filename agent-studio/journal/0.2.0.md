# 0.2.0 decision memo

Tonight is the studio's first patch, so it is a big one.
The headline is Evolutions, a new system.
The owner chose this as the first big patch: port the dry-run patches 0.2.0 and 0.2.1 onto the current game.

## What we chose

Each of Kael's spells now has an evolved form.
Learn the spell, raise its partner passive to full rank, reach level 6, and the next wave pick deals the evolution as a gold first card.
Fire Flask with Mana Font becomes Sunflask, a flask that bursts and throws three more at nearby foes.
Ice Spikes with Swift Sigils becomes Glacier Bloom, with a second line of spikes behind Kael and a spire that shatters into freezing shards.
Lightning with Arcane Power becomes Thunderhead, a storm cloud that calls three more bolts.

Ysra got her glaive, Heat and fire rites after the dry patches were written, so she has no spells to evolve.
We gave her one evolution of her own tonight.
Flame Pillar with Kindling at full rank becomes Pyre Colonnade: the pillar burns hotter, and two more pillars burst up on either side a beat later.
Kindling is her rite line's own passive, and our wave 20 bot already builds Flame Pillar with Kindling maxed, so the recipe matches how she is played.

Evolutions bring the Vampire Survivors build story into Warpblade.
A run now has a goal to build toward, and the price is real: points spent on a spell or rite line are points not spent on combo, movement or an ultimate.

We ship the dry 0.2.1 tuning straight away.
Sunflask's small flasks aim at nearby foes, and Thunderhead's follow-up bolts hit for 1.5 times ability power.
There is no reason to ship the weaker versions and fix them a day later.

## What rides along

A player reported a flash of unstyled page every time the game loads (item 4).
The game loads its stylesheets from JavaScript, so the page can paint before its styles arrive.
The stylesheets move into the page head, and the first paint becomes the game's dark background.

A jump pressed just after an attack started was lost, for both heroes, on keyboard and touch.
It now waits for the blow and jumps out of it.
Kael's held Up still aims Rising Launcher, and Ysra's Up with J still gives the Skyhook.

The Rift Warden's HP drops by 20 percent, from 2439 to 1951 at wave 20.
Our new wave 20 bot runs fall to it in about 35 seconds at level 14.
Coins drop 25 percent more often, since a run of 12 to 20 coins buys barely one shop level.

## What we did not choose

Evolutions for Ysra's Flare and Brazier, and for Kael's warp, blade and ultimates, are the next round.
Four new effects plus a port is a full night already.
We also left the Warden's damage alone tonight.
The bots fall early in fresh runs too, so we will measure the HP cut first and then decide.
Class advancement, achievements with a collection, and a second stage stay on the roadmap.

## What shipped

- Evolutions, a new system: from level 6, a learned skill with its partner passive at full rank is dealt as a gold first card at the next wave pick.
- Kael: Sunflask (Fire Flask with Mana Font 3/3), Glacier Bloom (Ice Spikes with Swift Sigils 3/3) and Thunderhead (Lightning with Arcane Power 4/4).
- Ysra: Pyre Colonnade (Flame Pillar with Kindling 3/3), main pillar 5.5 to 6.6 times ability power plus two flank pillars at 3.85 times.
- Recipe notes on the cards of each skill and its partner, a gold celebration with a banner, and a gold top edge on the evolved HUD tab.
- A jump pressed during a blow is kept until the blow can be left, on keyboard and touch, for both heroes; Kael's held Up waits for J with Rising Launcher, and Ysra's Up with J still gives the Skyhook.
- Rift Warden base HP 900 to 720 (wave 20: 2439 to 1951; wave 30: 3249 to 2599).
- Every foe drops 25 percent more coins, on top of Lodestone's bonus.
- The load flash fix (feedback 4): stylesheets and fonts load from each page head, and the first paint is the dark background.
- After the first QA round, the Thunderhead cloud moved down below the HUD and got a lit underside.

## QA verdict

The final QA round passed.
Typecheck, lint, build and the smoke check for both heroes passed with no page errors.
Every evolution gate, card, recipe note and celebration worked as planned at 1280 x 720 and 844 x 390, by tap, J, Enter and 1 to 3.
Jumps after J went from 12 of 20 (keyboard) and 9 of 20 (touch) for Kael on the base build to 20 of 20 for both heroes on both inputs.
Rising Launcher worked 8 of 8, and Ysra's Skyhook 15 of 16, the one miss starting from an air move.
A save from the base version loaded with its coins, purchases and options intact.
QA noted four minor issues for later.
Glacier Bloom and Sunflask add little against a lone foe up close.
The wave 20 bots still fall to the Rift Warden with most of its HP left.
The evolution banner, like the existing wave banner, is hard to read over the bright day sky.
