# 0.12.2 HUD above the stage: director's memo

Tonight is a small patch with no day work and no player feedback in triage.
The smoke check passed for all nine heroes with no page errors, so nothing is broken in the way the charter ranks first.

I chose the HUD.
Since 0.12.1, in small desktop windows such as 640 by 600, 800 by 600, 700 by 700 and 1280 by 1024, the HUD sits inside the stage and covers about the top 40% of the fight.
Flying foes pass behind it.
Meanwhile 120 to 150 pixels of empty room sit above the stage.
QA logged this as a minor issue on 0.12.1, and we shipped the related mid-run jump at 600 by 540 as a known issue in those notes.
Both are real problems players see in every fight at those sizes, and combat readability is our first pillar.

The first change adds a compact HUD layout that sits above the stage, either side of the pause button: two short columns of bars, one row of build slots, and the tabs, wave and ultimate on three short lines.
It is used only when the full HUD cannot fit above the stage but the compact one can.
Phones, tablets and large windows keep exactly what they have now.

The second change makes the HUD choose its place from its largest size in the run, with room kept for a full build strip and every tab.
A pick can then no longer move it into the stage mid-fight.

I weighed balance work and left it out tonight.
Wave 5 is still the wall: eight of nine skilled bots and all nine mash bots end there.
Oakheart was nerfed last patch, though, and our rule allows its next change in 0.13.1 at the earliest.
Easing the moths of that wave or healing before it would get around that rule and blur the measurement we need then.
The Rift Warden and the Gloam Shepherd need the late bot started at a real run's level, which is the owner's call.
The short ultimates and the over-band skills rest on single noisy room runs, and nerfing heroes while almost every run ends at wave 5 would make a hard game harder.

Two changes is a small patch, and I prefer two finished fixes to padding it with balance moves we cannot back with numbers.
Tomorrow is due as a big patch, and class advancement is first in line.

## What shipped

- `HudPlace` gains `"compact"`, and `hudPlace(stageTop, safeTop, heights)` tries the full HUD above the stage first (pause top 8 + pause 30 + gap 8 + height + gap 16), then the compact HUD (pause top + height + gap 16), then inside.
- `HUD_PLACE` adds `rounding: 1`, the pixel allowed when `fitStage` checks that each compact row stays inside its half of the window.
- The compact layout (`body[data-hud="compact"]`) uses 10 px text, 5 px bar tracks, 4 px pips, a 16 px label column, tab padding 0 3px, 12 px side margins or the safe area, and halves of 50vw minus 31 px around a 62 px middle for the pause button.
- In compact the bars sit in two columns of at most 150 px, the boss bar moves below the stage, and the co-op status line moves to the top of the stage.
- The HUD's sizes are now CSS variables on `.hud`, set once by the desktop, phone and compact layouts.
- `#buildReserve` holds an unseen strip of all 8 skill and 5 charm slots, and `#abilityReserve` an unseen row of every tab in the hero's kit, so the HUD's measured height is its largest in the run.
- `fitStage` measures both the full and the compact height on every fit, re-fits right away when `#abilityReserve` or `#ultName` changes, watches `.tab-reserve`, `.build-reserve` and `.ult` as well as `.hud`, and re-fits when the pixel fonts finish loading.
- The score line is split into `#downCount` and `#bestRun`, so compact can hide the best-run record.

The developer made three changes to the plan.
Compact shows only the down count and hides the best-run record, since the record grows mid-run and would overflow the right half.
Compact tabs use 0 3px padding with a 2 px gap and 4 px after WARP, so every hero's full tab row fits the 277 px half at 640 by 600.
At 640 by 600 and 700 by 700 a full build puts the charms on a second row under the skills, which still fits the 90 px budget (84 px at 640 by 600).

The reserved room also changes phones held upright: the HUD sits 47 px higher at 390 by 844 (top 163 px against 210 px), with an empty band under the bars early in a run, and at 320 by 568 it stays inside the stage for the whole run.

## Runtime dependencies

None were added.

## QA verdict

QA failed the first round and passed the second.
In the first round the compact HUD passed, but the place-fixed fix did not: at 600 by 540, Cerise, Vesper and Calder started compact and moved inside 6 to 11 s into wave 1.
The first fit chose compact before the hero's full tab row was measured, and a tab row that may not wrap overflows sideways without changing the HUD's height.
Commit 2eb3236 re-fits as soon as the hidden tab row or the ultimate name changes and watches the hidden rows' sizes.
In the second round the same three heroes kept the inside place from the start of the run to wave 5, and 9 heroes at 9 sizes from 500 by 480 to 640 by 600 showed no change of place after a forced resize.
Typecheck, lint and build pass, with only the chunk-size warning that was already there.
The smoke check passes for all nine heroes, 139 of 139 checks, with no page errors.
Skilled bot runs for all nine heroes match the baseline exactly, menus open with no errors, and a save from the base build loads with its coins and purchases.

QA listed four minor issues.
At 640 by 600 and 700 by 700 a full build puts the charms on a second row under the skills.
Large windows that now use compact, such as 1280 by 1024, 1440 by 900 and 1180 by 820, show the phone-size HUD in the top corners with room left unused.
On a phone held upright the HUD sits 47 px higher than in 0.12.1, with an empty reserved band early in a run, and at 320 by 568 it stays inside the stage.
Already in 0.12.1: in the day era the backdrop beside the stage shows lighter bands, and at 600 by 540 a second sun appears on the backdrop beside the stage's sun.
