# 0.14.0 decision memo: Bog Rush

Tonight is a big patch: 0.13.0 shipped on 21 November, three days ago, and there is no day work.
No feedback is waiting and no commits landed since 0.13.2.
The baseline smoke check passed for all nine heroes with no page errors, so there is no bug to fix first.

## What we chose

The headline is a new boss fight: the Bog Hulk at wave 10 becomes a two-phase boss.
At half HP it sinks into the bog for 1.6 seconds and takes half again as much damage.
Then it bursts back up with its mud cracked open on violet rift light.
In its second phase it keeps the faster pattern it already had and adds the Bog Rush.
The Bog Rush is a belly slide along a marked path: jump it or dodge it, then punish the Hulk while it lies stuck in the mud.
Slaying it now pays 10 coins, and a new achievement, Bog Breaker, asks for five Bog Hulk kills.

## Why

The Bog Hulk is where runs are ending now that Oakheart is easier.
In last night's skilled bot runs, the two heroes who reached wave 10, Vesper and Zafir, both fell to it, and Zafir's mash run did too.
It is also the last early leader with one phase, which the roadmap flagged after the Gloam Shepherd showed what a kneel and a second phase add.
A fight with a readable break in the middle and a move you answer with a jump is the Grand Chase boss pattern we want at every era's end.
The achievement gives players who already hold their heroes' Trials a reason to come back to the same boss.

## What we passed on

The new stage is still the bigger goal, but it needs eras, foes, hazards and an unlock, and that does not fit one night at the quality bar.
A second round of jobs and run modifiers are both new systems, and 0.13.0 was a new system, so we rotate away from that kind.
The Abyss Maw also has one phase, but almost no runs reach wave 25 yet.
New foes for the Abyss stay on the roadmap as a strong next pick.

## What stays the same

Phase 1 of the fight, the Bog Hulk's HP and the heroes' Trials do not change.
We left its stats alone so the bot numbers show what the new phase does by itself.
The new marks on its body are at least two art pixels wide and lit, because QA found Oakheart's one-pixel split hard to read.

## What shipped

- The Bog Hulk's tuning in one file, `src/game/enemies/rain/hulkMoves.ts`, with phase 1 numbers unchanged and the phase read from `e.phase`.
- The Sinking: at half HP any wind-up is dropped and the Hulk sinks for 1.6 s, with no guard, stuns dropped and 1.5 x damage taken, through the kind's `takes`, `steady` and `guarding` hooks.
- The Rise over 0.45 s, with a mud floor ring, a violet ring, a light and flash, 14 mud chunks, 8 embers, a shake and no damage.
- The phase 2 look: no mossy hood, three lit violet cracks that flare with each wind-up, two reeds, brighter eyes, rising embers, and a glow radius of 52 px in place of 40 through a new optional `glowRadius` hook on `EnemyKind`.
- The Bog Rush: a 0.9 s wind-up (scaled by the wave) with a violet dash mark over the full path, a 150 px belly slide at 170 px/s with a low 36 by 16 px hit box, 20 base damage once per hero per Rush, 1.1 s stuck afterwards and a wet streak that fades over 1.5 s.
- New drawing for the dive, slide and stuck poses, with the Bog Hulk's art split into `hulkStanding.ts`, `hulkSlidePose.ts`, `hulkCracks.ts`, `hulkLook.ts` and smaller files under `src/game/enemies/rain/`.
- `shift` and `clipBelow` on `SpriteCanvas`, for a body that sinks into the floor as one.
- Bog Hulk coins from 8 to 10 in `src/constants/coins.ts`.
- The Bog Breaker achievement (`boghulk`, slay the Bog Hulk 5 times, 50 coins, hidden until Night Watch), taking the page to 42 achievements and 3,010 coins.
- The README, `docs/achievements.md`, `docs/combo-design.md` and `docs/graphics.md` updated to match.

Plan deviation, from the developer report and QA's first pass.
The plan chose the Rush only with the hero 60 to 170 px away and then half the time, and QA's first pass found it almost never happened against melee heroes.
In 55cf009f the Hulk always chooses the Rush when its timer is ready and the hero is on the ground, at any distance, sliding through the hero from close range.
When a wall leaves it less than 40 px to slide, it wades back facing the hero for up to 1.2 s first.
The gap timer now starts at launch, so an interrupted slide cannot Rush again at once.

## Runtime dependencies

None were added.
The patch does not change `package.json`.

## Balance

Full-run skilled and mash bots for all nine heroes match the baseline exactly.
At wave 10, level 12, the skilled bot matches the base for 7 of 9 heroes, and Aldric and Brynn fell 3 to 4 s later.
Mash at wave 10, level 12 is the same except Kael, who now falls at wave 10 where the base cleared it, with 0 HP lost to the Rush in QA's replay, so QA reads it as random-number noise.
In QA's player scenario at wave 10, level 16, Kael, Brynn and Cerise each met 4 Rushes in about 40 s of phase 2, up from 0 before the fix.
A Bog Rush on a hero standing still at wave 10 costs 29 HP.
In the level 30 bot fights most heroes end phase 2 within 4 to 8 s, before the first Rush is due.
The Bog Hulk drops 10 coins as designed, and the run counts 12 or 13 with the global 25 percent coin bonus.

## QA verdict

QA failed the first pass (qa-0) with one major issue: the Bog Rush almost never happened in ordinary fights.
The developer fixed it in 55cf009f, and QA passed the second pass (qa-1).
Typecheck, lint and build pass, and the smoke check passes 139 checks for all nine heroes with no page errors.
The Sinking, the Rise, the phase 2 look at phone and desktop size, the phase 2 attacks, the stuck window, the streak and the Rush spacing were all checked in play.
A close-range Rush is fair: a jump in the last 0.4 to 0.5 s of the wind-up always clears it.
The wall wade, the adventure room, a two-seat co-op room, the Bog Breaker achievement and a save from 0.13.2 all passed.

QA left four minor issues.
The Rush path dashes are faint at phone width, and at close range they start under the hero's feet.
The acceptance check for 10 coins in the run count reads 12 or 13 because of the coin bonus; the base drop is 10 as designed.
Kael's wave 10 mash bot now falls where the base cleared it, which looks like noise from the shared random generator.
A live two-browser co-op room over PeerJS is still untested.
