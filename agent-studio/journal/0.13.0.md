# 0.13.0 Prism Knight: director's memo

Tonight's patch is 0.13.0, a big patch, and its headline is class advancement.
Kael gets the first second job in the game, the Prism Knight.

The roadmap had class advancement first in line, and the last big patch was a new boss, so a new system keeps the rotation.
It brings the game closer to Grand Chase, where a hero grows by changing jobs.
It also gives every player a next goal, which is pillar 4.

The Prism Knight opens when Kael defeats Oakheart.
The roadmap first suggested the hero's Trial at the Bog Hulk.
In last night's bot runs only one hero reached the Bog Hulk, and Kael fell at Oakheart, so we chose a goal that real runs can reach.

The job changes the look, the end of the string and the ultimate.
Kael wears a pale violet coat with a crystal pauldron, and his trails turn to a new prism colour.
His third blow becomes a long thrust that lodges a prism shard in the foe.
His finisher becomes an overhead cut that shatters every shard nearby for extra damage.
Crystal Nova keeps its name and every skill value, and as the Prism Knight it also fires six prism beams that lodge shards.

We kept the Warp Knight exactly as it plays today.
Without shards the Prism Knight's string deals a little less than the Warp Knight's, so the job is a different way to play and does not simply outgrow the base job.
Skills that replace blows of the string still win over the job, so every build that works today still works.

We scoped it to one hero so the system and the job can both reach the quality bar tonight.
Co-op rooms play the base job for now, and carrying the choice over the network is on the roadmap.

We weighed a second stage, new boss phases, co-op round two and run modifiers.
The owner rebuilt the solo stage as a side-scrolling adventure yesterday, and that ships tonight as day work, so another stage can wait until this one is played and measured.
The owner's commit carries no headline mark, so it ships beside the studio's headline, and the patch notes cover it in full.
New boss phases would repeat the last headline.
Oakheart stays as it is until 0.13.1 under our rule against changing the same thing twice within three patches.

No feedback was waiting tonight.
The baseline smoke check passed for all nine heroes with no page errors, so there was no bug to fix first.

## What shipped

- A job system for heroes: `JobDef` (`src/types/job.ts`), optional `baseJob` and `jobs` on `CharacterDef`, and `heroJob()` (`src/game/hero/job.ts`) kept per seat.
- Job unlocks found through the same goal check as charms, saved in `meta.unlocks`, announced with a new `job` discovery ("New job" over the name) and listed on the run result.
- The chosen job per hero kept under the new local storage key `warpblade-jobs` by `src/store/jobs.ts` and `src/services/jobChoice.ts`, falling back to the base job when the saved one is locked or unknown.
- A JOB row on the hero screen with one chip per job, a padlock and hint on a locked chip, keyboard and touch control, and the portrait, roster tile, hero behind the menu, title and blurb following the chosen job.
- Kael's Prism Knight (`job-kael-prism`), opened by defeating Oakheart with Kael (`heroWave` kael 5).
- The Prism Knight's look in `src/heroes/kael/look/prism/`: pale violet coat, violet scarf, silver trim, pale blue eyes, a crystal pauldron and the new `PAL.prism` ramp and `SPARKS.prism`.
- Prism shards (`k_prism`): at most 3 per foe, gone 6 s after the newest, blinking in the last second, lodged by Prism Thrust, Warp Strike's arrival, the parry's riposte and the Nova beams.
- Prism Thrust (1.5 x attack, one hit, in place of Twin Fang's 0.75 + 0.85) and Shatterfall (2.1 x attack, in place of the falling smash's 2.35).
- Any ground string finisher as the Prism Knight shatters shards within 56 px for 0.55 x attack per shard, source attacks, able to crit.
- Crystal Nova as the Prism Knight fires six prism beams 0.12 s after the ring, each dealing 0.2 x the ring's blow and lodging a shard.
- Prism Knight lines for Attack and Guard on the Controls screen and the touch hints, and a 16 by 16 `kael_prism` icon.
- `?job=prism` on the dev server for playtest runs.
- Co-op host runs reset every seat to the base job.
- The README, `docs/hero-module.md`, `docs/heroes/kael.md` and `docs/unlocks.md` updated for jobs.
- Day work from the owner (8aa5370): the side-scrolling solo adventure with seven boss rooms, patrols, ledges, climbable walls, spike traps, mentors and an E to S grade on the solo results.

Plan deviations, from the developer report.
`baseJob` is optional, since making it required would have meant editing the other eight heroes' folders.
The pauldron is drawn in the Prism Knight's own arm part at about 4 by 5 art pixels, where the plan said an `overTorso` extra of about 7 by 5, because the sword arm covered an extra and a wider pauldron read as a chest plate.
A save that already meets the goal opens the job at load without a banner, the same as charms found at load.

## Runtime dependencies

None were added.
The patch does not change `package.json`.

## Balance

Skilled and mash bot runs for all nine heroes match the baseline exactly, since bots play the base job in fresh storage.
The Prism Knight was not measured by bot.
On paper his 3rd blow and finisher deal 3.6 x attack with no shards and 4.15 with his own thrust's shard, against 3.95 for the Warp Knight.

## QA verdict

QA passed the first round (qa-0).
Typecheck, lint and build pass, with only the existing Vite chunk-size warning.
The smoke check passes 139 checks for all nine heroes with no page errors.
The job row, keyboard and touch choice, reload, phone and desktop layouts, the string, shards, skill overrides, warp and parry shards, the Nova beams and the planned numbers were all checked in play.
A real Oakheart kill in a non-playtest solo run showed the banner and saved the unlock.
A save from the base build loads with every key and value unchanged.
Skilled, mash and skilled-from-wave-20 bots match the baseline for all nine heroes.
The adventure day work played with no page errors, and the secret works.

QA left five minor issues.
Crystal Nova's level beam runs at chest height and passes over grounded thornbacks and mires.
The co-op lobby shows Kael in the Prism Knight look when that job was chosen in solo, while the room plays the Warp Knight.
The pauldron is about 4 by 5 pixels and barely reads on the pale coat at game scale.
Oakheart's front guard blocks many Prism Thrusts, so shards build slowly on the boss the job is tied to.
A full co-op room with a real guest and the run-result "New job" entry after a real Oakheart kill were not verified in a browser.
