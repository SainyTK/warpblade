# 0.11.2 Kept presses: director's memo

Tonight is a small patch with no day work and no player feedback in triage.

The baseline smoke check passed for all nine heroes with no page errors, and the commits since 0.11.1 are empty, so there is no new breakage to fix first.

## What I chose

Four bug fixes that finish the work 0.11.1 started for Saya.
A press in Warpblade is kept for 0.15 s.
In 0.11.1 Saya lost presses because her own moves lasted about 0.7 s and nothing could cut into them.
Reading the code tonight, five more heroes have the same gap: their abilities run through states that no shared action may cut until they end.
Kael's and Yun's spells use the shared cast, which lasts 0.48 s.
Aldric's Shield Rush lasts 0.50 s and his calls of light 0.47 s.
Vesper's woven abilities last 0.43 to 0.53 s.
Calder's shots last 0.47 to 0.63 s and his reload 0.60 s.
A U, warp or guard pressed early in any of these is dropped.

Each fix follows Saya's: the move may be cut once it has landed (the spell has left the hand, the rush has stopped travelling, the round has fired), and a press made before that moment waits for it.
A tapped K is still not kept, so a parry never comes later than it was pressed; a held K raises the guard at the cut point.
Damage, costs, recharges and timings stay the same when nothing is pressed.

## Why this and not the wave 5 wall

The loudest number in the baseline is still wave 5: every skilled and every mash bot fell there or earlier.
Every skilled bot killed all 18 foes before Oakheart, the 4 moths included, and several entered wave 5 nearly whole, so Oakheart alone ends these runs.
The only lever that would move that number is Oakheart itself.
The charter does not allow the same thing to be nerfed twice within three patches, and 0.11.1 nerfed Oakheart's HP and sweep.
Raising the HP heroes carry in would not change runs where heroes already arrive nearly whole.
So the next Oakheart step waits for 0.11.4, with a time-to-kill measurement written into the roadmap now.

## Other options I weighed

The HUD that sits partly above the screen at some desktop sizes is a real bug, but the last attempt broke phones and it needs its own night of fit tests.
The Rift Warden cannot be tuned from a wave 20 bot that starts at level 14 when a real run is near level 35 there.
Skill bands and evolutions need the measuring room before any number moves.

## How it fits the charter

Pillar 1, combat first: a press that does what the player asked is the base of a responsive sword string, and Kael is the charter's bar for fun.
The fixes stay inside each hero's folder, except the shared cast rule, which lives with the shared cast in `src/game/hero`.

## What shipped

- The shared cast (Kael, Yun, and Cerise's and Saya's spells) may be cut by the warp, guard or ultimate once the spell has left the hand at 0.25 s of 0.48 s, and the dodge still cuts it at any time.
- A dash, warp, cast, wide cast or ultimate press made during the shared cast waits for that moment (`keptPresses` now covers the shared cast for kits without their own `holdsSharedPresses`).
- A kept press now stays one step short of the buffer, so it is still there on the step after the state that kept it ends; without this a second ability pressed during a move was dropped as the move settled.
- Aldric: Shield Rush may be cut once its travel ends (0.28 s of 0.50 s), and a call of light once the light has gone (0.20 s of 0.47 s), and presses wait through both.
- Aldric: a cut Shield Rush stops on the ground with no slide.
- Vesper: a woven ability may be cut once it has left her hands (0.17 s of 0.43 s, held 0.23 s of 0.53 s), the Stitch cannot be cut, and presses wait through both.
- Vesper: her own ability rule no longer cuts the Stitch mid-travel or replaces a woven ability before it has left her hands, a bug found while checking her doc.
- Calder: a shot may be cut once its rounds are out (0.17 s, Twin Scatter 0.33 s), including during Scatter Blast's hop, which the cut ends as a landing would.
- Calder: the reload may be cut by any shared action once the rounds are in (0.42 s of 0.60 s), and presses wait through shots and the reload.
- Calder: an I pressed during a reload before the rounds are in waits and fires as they snap in, through a new optional `paidSoon` hook on `AbilitySlot`; the shot tabs stay struck through until then.
- Brynn: Rend and Quake keep a shared press until the blow has left the blade.
- Cerise: Flurry, Rose Line, the riposte and her spells keep a shared press until it may cut them, and her spells may be cut once they let loose.
- Saya: her spells may be cut once they have left the blade, and a cut before the click plays the click so stored cuts still release.
- U pressed 0.1 s after I now starts the ultimate at: Kael 352 ms, Yun 352 ms, Aldric 384 ms, Brynn 336 ms, Cerise 512 ms, Saya 288 ms, Vesper 256 ms and Calder 256 ms, where each was dropped before; Zafir's kit is untouched and starts it at 208 ms.
- The hero docs and `docs/moveset-design.md` describe the cut points and say another ability waits until the hero is free, except for Saya, whose abilities cut at the same moment.

The plan said Calder's shots should never be cut during the Scatter Blast hop.
The hop lasts almost the whole shot and locked out every action, so the developer let the cut end the hop as a landing would; Scatter Blast then U went from 624 ms to 256 ms.
The plan did not name Brynn, Cerise or Saya, but checking every kit showed they kept no press through their own states or the shared cast, so they got the same fix.

## Runtime dependencies

None were added.
The patch does not change `package.json`.

## QA verdict

QA passed the patch on its fourth round, on commit fcd441d.
Typecheck, lint and build pass, with only the chunk-size warning that was already there.
The smoke check passes for all nine heroes, 139 checks, with no page errors.
In the browser, every hero's ultimate pressed 0.1 s after I starts at the developer's reported time, Vesper's Stitch runs to its end with an ability pressed during it, and Calder's I during a reload fires as the rounds snap in with the tabs struck through.
The mash bots match the baseline exactly.
On the skilled bots eight heroes still fall at wave 5, Vesper now reaches wave 10 with 47 kills, and Calder reaches wave 5 with 17 kills against 14.
Menus open with no errors and a save from the base build loads with its coins and purchases.

QA listed three minor issues.
When Calder knows a shell, an I pressed during a reload moves the selection to the shell, as on base, and now fires the shell as the rounds load; QA suggests that `pressAbility` wait for a selected `paidSoon` slot before falling through.
Vesper's skilled bot reaching wave 10 needs no fix, but it should be checked when Oakheart is retuned in 0.11.4.
The start menu still shows v0.11.1 until the release step sets the version.
