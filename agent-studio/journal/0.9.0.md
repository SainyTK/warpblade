## The hero: Jory, the war drummer

The roadmap's suggested order puts the rhythm fighter next, and the day work made it a better fit tonight than before.
Every hero's string now plays the same every time at an even pace, which gives a beat-driven hero a steady floor to stand on.
Jory fights with two mallets and a war drum, and his whole kit runs on one beat that the player can see at his feet, feel in his body and hear as a soft tick.
Every press of J, K, L and I is judged against that beat.
On the beat his blows are Accents that hit 1.4 times as hard, his Boom pushes foes away and drowns their shots, and his Beat Step is untouchable.
Each on-beat press adds a pip of Groove, and Groove raises his tempo from 96 to 132 beats a minute, so keeping time makes him faster and longer reaching.
Mashing never builds Groove, and he then plays as an ordinary heavy hitter at the slowest tempo.

He sits apart from every hero.
Kael's Keen timing is a short window after his own blow and only adds a crit; Jory's beat is one clock for the run that judges his defence and travel too.
Ysra parries one blow from the front, and Jory clears space all around him.
Orrin earns his resource from speed and Mirel from waiting; Jory earns his from timing, and it changes how fast he acts.
Tallis wins by where he stands, and Jory wins by when he presses.

The scope is a full kit with 20 skills, his look, his achievements and touch controls, as each sprint night has shipped.
I cut a beat offset setting for audio delay and a gait-model walk to the roadmap, since the visual cues need no calibration and his Steady Hand ability widens the window for players who need it.

## What I passed on

The berserker is the most distinct direction left, and I kept it for the tenth hero.
I also considered blows that wait to land on the beat, as some rhythm action games do, and dropped the idea because a press could wait over half a second at his slowest tempo.
Tallis now falls two waves before everyone else on the mash bot, and every hero still falls to the Rift Warden at the wave 20 start.
Both need a measured look on their own night, and both are on the roadmap with tonight's numbers.

## What shipped

- Jory, the war drummer (studio, new-hero headline): 1800 coins, 105 HP, 12 attack, 8 ability power, 85 MP, 110 stamina, 4% crit, move 1.0, one jump of the shared height.
- The beat: a clock per seat at 96, 108, 120 or 132 beats a minute by Groove tier, judged within 0.1 s, shown by the floor ring, his body, the GV bar's lamp and a soft tick.
- Groove on the new GV bar: 12 pips, +1 on the beat, -3 off it, -4 when hurt, -1 a beat after four silent beats while foes stand.
- The mallet string Tap, Roll and Crash, the same every time, with Accents at 1.4x (1.6x at Crescendo), Crash's reach and sound wave growing by tier, and Downstroke in the air.
- Boom on K, Beat Step on L, Steady Hand and Thunder Skin on I, and Drumroll on U, which leaves him at 8 Groove or more with 8 beats of grace.
- A J pressed in the flinch is judged at once, and a late one opens the string with Tap on landing (415b1c8, after QA's round two).
- 20 skills with evolutions and code-drawn icons, Jory's Trial and In Time (achievements go from 31 worth 2,370 coins to 33 worth 2,470), the tracked stat `crescendoSeconds`, his keys on the Controls screen, his touch buttons, per-seat co-op memory, and Aftershock at the end of every Beat Step.
- Shared code: `src/game/beat.ts` (`advanceBeats`, called from `stepGame`) keeps the beat through hit-stop and slow motion; the `drawFloor` kit hook, the `dr` pose key, the `drum` and `headband` skin flags, and `sfx.tone`'s start delay do nothing for other heroes.
- Co-op: the host now measures each guest's round trip on every heartbeat, and a guest Jory is judged within 0.14 s against the beat shifted by that trip plus a video frame.
- The skilled bot page close was traced to the harness, with no change to the game: Tallis's skilled run stalls on wave 4 until its 30 game-minute limit, the studio step's time limit closed the page during it, and the game never hung, crashed or grew in memory.
- Day work (new-boss headline, 5a9f3d2): the Abyss Sovereign at wave 30 with 800 base HP and 40 coins, The Unblinking after it with an achievement worth 500 coins, jumps of one height however long Up is held, picks that open 0.9 s after the last foe falls, a held skill's next level weighted 9 in place of 6, and Last Light in three levels for 300, 480 and 770 coins.
- Day work (animation-rework headline, 12cba43): the fight at 80% of its old pace, struck minions that stagger with their action cut, a guard broken on the second blow, every hero's string the same every time with rekeyed blows, jumps that load for two frames, and the hit counter.
- Day work (effect-rework headline, b8ec2bc): every era's scene at full detail, redrawn foes, finer hit effects and hero skills, soft shadows, capped stacked light, and frames that cost about 45% less in the busiest fights.
