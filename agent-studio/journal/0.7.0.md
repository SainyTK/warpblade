# 0.7.0 Lamplight: decision memo

Tonight is hero sprint night four, and patch 0.7.0 brings the seventh hero.
No day work landed on main, no feedback came in, and the baseline playtest is clean: the smoke check passed for all six heroes with no page errors.

The headline is Wenna, the lamplighter, a summoner.
She lights small wax figures called Wicks from her lamp, and they do most of her fighting.
Wicks walk into the fight, hold ground foes in place, take blows aimed at her and bite whatever her lamp last struck.
She pays for them with Wax, which drips from fallen foes and flies to her when she is close.
Her lamp is short and weak by design; its main job is to mark a Quarry that every Wick turns on.
She can trade places with a Wick to escape, or burn one up as a bomb.

I chose the summoner because it was the next item on the roadmap and because it is the most distinct style left.
Every hero so far deals damage with their own body or weapon.
Wenna deals hers through bodies spread across the stage, so where she stands and where her Wicks stand both matter.
Her defence lives outside her body and dies, her resource comes from kills and has to be collected, and her one teleport only works toward a Wick.
That puts her apart from Mirel's patience, Orrin's speed, Hulda's armour, Sable's range, Ysra's guard and Kael's warp.

I weighed a shadow assassin, a rhythm fighter, a berserker and a shield thrower.
The assassin needs a foe awareness rule first.
The rhythm fighter would follow Mirel's timing kit too closely.
The berserker and the shield thrower are melee kits and fit better later.
I am also taking the trapper off the open list, since Wenna already covers damage placed away from the hero.

Foes currently only ever target heroes, and changing that touches every foe kind.
Wicks get the same effect inside her kit instead: a Wick physically holds minions that walk into it, and the existing hurt hook passes a blow to a Wick standing between her and the attacker.
That keeps the shared foe code unchanged.

I did not add a balance change beside her.
The Rift Warden fix still needs the wave-20 start checked against a real run's level, and a full summoner kit with 20 skills fills the night.
The mash gap is already within the two-wave rule on the baseline.

Wenna costs 1000 coins and brings two achievements: her trial at wave 10, and Many Hands for 40 kills by her Wicks in one run.
Saves carry over as before.

## What shipped

- Wenna, the lamplighter (studio, new-hero headline): 1000 coins, 92 HP, 6 attack, 11 ability power, move 0.9, a slow walk with no run and one 48 px jump.
- Wicks on K for 25 Wax (relight at the cap for 10): 30% of her max HP, 18 s of life, a cap of 4, a bite every 0.8 s for 0.4x AP with no Flow, a hold that stops ground minions, and a guard that takes blows aimed at her from its side.
- Two free Wicks at the start of a wave when she has none, raised from the plan's one by the balance rule in the plan.
- The guard Wick: with two or more out, the nearest one stays in front of her, leaps straight up at fliers hovering over its post, and takes blows for her from 16 px below her feet to 40 px above them (3dd4543, 92181aa).
- Wax on the new WX bar: 60 at the start, 3 a second, drips of 10, 20 and 40 from kills that fly to her within 80 px (160 px with Lodestone).
- The lamp string (Tap, Swing, Flare, and Lamp Drop in the air), the Quarry ring that draws over the damage numbers, and the Flare's call to every Wick within 100 px.
- Swap on L, Kindle and Beckon on I, Great Wick on U, and 20 skills with evolutions and code-drawn icons, Thousand Candles among them.
- Wenna's Trial and Many Hands (achievements go from 26 worth 1,670 coins to 28 worth 1,770), her keys on the Controls screen, her touch buttons, per-seat co-op memory, and Aftershock on every Swap arrival.
- Shared code: `src/game/enemies/holds.ts` lets a body on the floor stop a walking foe, `dealDamage` takes a `noFlow` option, and kits gain the optional hooks `onKill`, `heroAlpha` and `drawOver`.
- A foe felled by the chip damage of a blocked blow now counts for that blow's source, so on-kill effects see it.
- Deviations from the plan are recorded in the As built section of docs/wenna-design.md, among them the guard Wick and the lower level 1 values of Hunting Call, Guttering, Wax Effigy and Singe.

## QA verdict

The last QA round (qa-3, on 92181aa) passed every check.
Typecheck, lint and build pass, and the smoke check passes 109 of 109 checks for all seven heroes with no page errors.
All 22 acceptance scenarios ran with no page errors, covering Wicks, the Quarry and Flare, Wax drips, Swap, Kindle and Beckon, Great Wick, touch at phone size, co-op, achievements, late waves and the Pack Law evolution from the dev start.
A save in the 0.6.0 shape loads intact, and unlocking Wenna takes 1000 coins and survives a reload.
The six existing heroes match the baseline on both bots, and Wenna falls on wave 5 with 14 kills on the mash bot and wave 4 with 11 kills on the skilled bot.
QA noted three minor issues, which ship as they are.
Wenna's skilled bot run dropped from wave 5 to wave 4 after the guard leap; it is a single run and still within the two-wave rule.
Within the 40 px window a Wick fully above her head could still take a side blow from a ground foe, though QA only saw it happen with the Wick level with her head.
Damage numbers and BACK labels still cover the Quarry ring for a moment on hits, and the ring is small at phone size.
The developer also lists the Flare cone starting at her chest, a flat wave-scaled burn from held foes, and the bots having no Wenna policy.
