# 0.14.2 decision memo: Oakheart and the Bog Hulk ease up

Tonight is a small patch with no player feedback in the queue and no commits since 0.14.1, so the plan comes from the bots and from QA's open items.
The smoke check passed for all nine heroes with no page errors, so there is nothing broken to fix first.

The bot numbers say two era leaders end almost every run.
At wave 5, five of nine skilled heroes fall to Oakheart, and only Aldric and Calder get past it.
At wave 10, no hero at level 12 clears the Bog Hulk in either bot.

For Oakheart I chose the lever the roadmap already named: its rest between attacks once enraged goes from 0.65 to 0.8 of the base.
It was last eased in 0.13.1, three patches ago, so the charter allows it, and the change stays under the 25 percent cap.

For the Bog Hulk the roadmap said to tune the Rush first.
I went back to the scenario the 0.14.0 developer ran and found that eight of nine heroes died before the Hulk ever sank, with half or more of its HP left, and none saw a Rush.
Tuning the Rush would change nothing a level 12 hero meets, so the tune moves to the first phase.
It goes in steps with a measurement after each: a longer rest before it sinks, then lighter slams, mud waves and globs, then HP only if the first two are not enough.
The target is two to five of nine skilled heroes clearing wave 10, so the Hulk stays a wall worth respecting.

Two fixes close QA's open items from 0.14.1.
The level 1 badge in the narrowest compact HUD will render as crisp as the other digits.
The lint rule that keeps drawing code away from the play random numbers will also catch a namespace import, which today slips past it.

I left the wave 20 bosses alone because the late bot starts far below a real run's level, and I kept the HUD layout ideas and the chunk split for later.

## What shipped

- Oakheart's rest between attacks above half HP is 1.5 times the base, up from 1.25 (`OAK_CALM_REST`).
- Oakheart's rest once enraged is 0.8 of the base, up from 0.65, now the named constant `OAK_ENRAGED_REST`.
- The Bog Hulk rests 1.25 times the base in phase 1 (`HULK_COOLDOWN.calm`), up from 1; phase 2 stays at 0.7.
- The Bog Hulk's base hits are slam 18, mud wave 14 and glob 11, down from 22, 18 and 14; the Bog Rush stays at 20.
- The Bog Hulk's base HP is 350, down from 430, about 3,724 to 3,031 at wave 10.
- The compact HUD badge under 273 px has a fixed 6 px width with right-aligned text, so the narrow '1' lands on whole pixels.
- The `src/scene/**` override in `biome.json` sets `performance/noNamespaceImport` to error, and `docs/graphics.md` says so.

Plan deviations from the developer report.
The plan used `style/noNamespaceImport`, which Biome 2.6 does not have, so the rule is `performance/noNamespaceImport`.
The plan kept every Oakheart number but the enraged rest.
With the enraged rest alone, skilled read 2, 2 and 5 of 9 past wave 5 across the three seeds, and a scenario on the default seed showed the heroes who fall there (Vesper, Yun, Calder) die with Oakheart at 57 to 85 percent HP, before it enrages.
So the developer also raised the calm rest from 1.25 to 1.5, +20 percent and under the cap.
All three Bog Hulk steps shipped, because the calm rest alone left 0 of 9 skilled heroes clearing wave 10.

## Runtime dependencies

None were added.
The patch does not change `package.json`.

## QA verdict

QA failed the first round (qa-0) because the planned Bog Hulk hit and HP cuts were missing although their conditions were met, and the calm rest and Oakheart's enraged rest alone did not move the bots enough.
QA passed the second round (qa-1).
Typecheck, lint and build pass, with only the known Vite chunk-size warning.
The smoke check passes for all nine heroes with no page errors.
QA's own deterministic runs reproduce the developer's Oakheart and Hulk numbers hero by hero, and runs that end before wave 5 match the baseline.
In a copy, a namespace import of game state under `src/scene` fails lint, the named `rng` import still fails, and the same import under `src/ui` passes.
At 560, 600 and 620 by 600 at 1x density the '1' badge sits on whole pixels, with its size and place unchanged.
A 0.14.1 save loads with its coins and shop items, and every menu screen opens without errors.

QA left five minor issues.
Oakheart's calm rest moved from 1.25 to 1.5 beyond the plan, which the charter allows and the notes and roadmap list.
The median Hulk HP left is 52 percent, above the 40 percent target, and mash still clears wave 10 with 0 of 9 heroes, so the Kael wave 10 mash item stays open.
The scene lint rule does not catch a dynamic `import()` of game state, and its error message is generic.
The 8 px badges in the regular compact HUD, 640 px wide and up, sit on fractional pixels and look slightly soft, as before this patch.
Seed copies with a symlinked `node_modules` get font 403s, so layout checks on copies are unreliable; bot results are unaffected.
