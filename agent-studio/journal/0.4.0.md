# 0.4.0 decision memo

Tonight's patch is 0.4.0, a big patch, and the first night of the hero sprint.
The owner's day work ships in it: Sable the hooded hunter, achievements with coin rewards, coin unlocks for heroes, and a hero screen built for ten heroes.
The charter asks for one new hero every night until there are ten, so the studio adds a fourth: Hulda, the iron grappler.

Hulda fights with her hands in iron gauntlets.
She grabs a foe and holds it, knees it, and throws it by the direction held: forward into other foes, a suplex into the floor behind her, or a toss straight up for a juggle.
In the air she catches fliers and drives them into the floor.
She has no dodge and no block.
Grabbing, throwing and taking blows build Grit, and every 25 Grit is an armour plate that halves a blow and lets her keep going.
Bellow pulls foes toward her, Bull Rush ploughs through them, and a taunt turns a quiet moment into armour.

She was chosen because she moves and defends unlike anyone in the roster.
Kael escapes through untouchable dashes and warps, Ysra blocks from the front and vaults over danger, and Sable keeps foes away with bolts and hops.
Hulda walks toward danger, is never untouchable, and has the shortest reach in the game, with thrown foes as her only projectile.
Grab and throw is a shape of attack the game has not had, and it leans on the Grand Chase reference: holds, throws and armour on heavy moves.

We weighed a summoner, a trapper, a berserker and a shadow assassin.
The trapper sits too close to Sable's snares.
The berserker would be a heavy weapon on a familiar swing loop, which the charter rules out.
The summoner and the assassin each need new foe behaviour (ally AI, foes losing track of a hero) that one night cannot build to the quality bar.
Moving foes with the grappler's hold and thrown body will make a summoner easier later.

No feedback was waiting, and the baseline playtest found no errors.
The day work already fills the patch, so tonight adds no balance changes.
The Rift Warden at wave 20 still beats every hero in about 30 seconds in the bot runs, and it stays on the roadmap for a quieter night.

Hulda costs 400 coins by the price rule, comes with her own trial achievement, and gets a real benefit from every shop item.

## What shipped

- Hulda, the iron grappler (studio, new-hero headline): 400 coins, 120 HP, move 0.9, a four-blow fist string, grab and crush, knees, forward throw, suplex, Sky Toss, Skyfall Grab, Bull Rush, Bellow, taunt, Quake Drop and Titan Throw, and a skill tree of Brawl, Iron, Hold and Fusion.
- Grit and armour plates for Hulda: four plates of 25, one at the start of a run, a plated blow deals 50% (40% with Iron Plates) with no knockback and 0.8 s of safety.
- Hulda's Trial achievement, her keys on the Controls screen, her touch buttons, and co-op claims on held foes.
- Deviations from the plan, recorded in docs/hulda-design.md: Grit per landed J blow is 4 (plan 3), plate safety is 0.8 s (plan 0.35 s), the Haymaker and Uppercut reach 42 and 44 px up and the Haymaker lunges in, and Bellow drags fliers down to her hands for 0.9 s.
- Sable, the hooded hunter (day work acfbd45, new-hero headline): a repeating crossbow with an 8-bolt quiver and a timed reload, a kick, a hop back that shoots, a slide, snares, Bolt Storm and Deadeye, at 300 coins.
- Achievements and coin unlocks (day work da1a802, new-system headline): 22 achievements worth 1,470 coins, a banner when one is earned, and coin prices for every hero after Ysra.
- The hero screen as a roster of tiles over one panel for the hero in focus, with shop bonuses drawn on the stat bars (day work 3b38ff4).
- Warp Shock renamed Aftershock and made to answer every hero's travel moves (day work fe021a2).
- Google Analytics page views and run start and end events from warpblade.sainytk.com only (day work 039b4af and 1f724da).

## QA verdict

The final QA round (qa-2) passed at ffa52e8.
Typecheck, lint and build pass, and the smoke check passes for Kael, Ysra, Sable and Hulda with no page errors.
Bot runs for Kael, Ysra and Sable match the baseline exactly, and Hulda falls on mash wave 4 with 10 kills and skilled wave 4 with 11 kills, within two waves of the others.
Grabs, knees, all throws, Crush on an Oakheart, Bellow then grab on fliers (8 of 8), Quake Drop, Titan Throw, the skill tree, the Controls and Achievements screens, touch at phone size and co-op all passed in the running game.
Saves from the base commit and from 0.3.0 load with coins, shop levels, Sable and achievements intact, and Hulda locked at 400.
The Last Light fix (322c8ec) and the head repaint (ffa52e8) passed.
QA noted three minor issues.
Plate safety is 0.8 s against the plan's 0.35 s, a recorded deviation backed by bot numbers.
Skyfall Grab rarely reaches moths with a tapped W (1 in 22), while Bellow then a grab works every time.
Hulda's grey crest still reads a little as a skullcap and her stubble as freckles at game scale.
