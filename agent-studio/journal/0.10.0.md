# 0.10.0 Red Oath: decision memo

Tonight is the last night of the hero sprint, so the headline is the tenth hero.
The base build is clean: the smoke check passed for all nine heroes with no page errors, and there are no commits since 0.9.0 and no player feedback to triage.
With nothing broken and nothing asked for, the decision came down to which play style is furthest from the nine we have.

We chose the berserker, Brannoc.
The roadmap had narrowed the tenth slot to the berserker or the shield thrower.
A returning shield would sit near Orrin's chain and Sable's bolts, and blocking with it would sit near Ysra's brace.
No hero pays for anything with HP, so the berserker changes the resource axis outright.

His HP is his resource.
Frenzy on K and his abilities on I cost HP, and the HP he spends becomes a debt he wins back by landing blows.
If a foe lands a blow first, the debt is gone.
Every point of HP he is missing makes him hit harder and swing faster, and below 35% his Cleave spins twice.
He has no block, no parry, no armour and no untouchable travel, and his only lunge goes toward foes.
His special, Berserk, gives him a few seconds where he cannot fall, which is his answer to the bosses.

Hulda was the closest neighbour, since she also takes blows.
She halves them with armour plates and keeps her HP.
Brannoc takes every blow in full and turns the loss into damage, so the two play the fight the opposite way round.

We left out a balance fix tonight.
Jory's flinch timing, Tallis's mash regression and the Rift Warden at wave 20 all need measurement before a change.
A hero with 20 skills, evolutions, icons, touch controls and co-op memory already fills the night.
They lead the small patch list now that the sprint is over.

After this patch the game has ten heroes and big patches return to the three-day cadence, with class advancement first in line.

## What shipped

- Brannoc, the berserker (studio, new-hero headline): 2450 coins, 125 HP, 13 attack, 6 ability power, 70 MP, 100 stamina, 6% crit, move 1.02, attack speed 0.9, one jump of the shared height.
- Fury: damage up to +60%, attack rate up to +30% and walk up to +15% in proportion to missing HP; Bloodied at 35% HP or less, with a second Cleave spin, a 3% heal on kills and his own Bloodied idle and look.
- The debt: HP spent on K and I, capped at 40% of max HP, won back at 2.5% of max HP per foe struck (5% on a crit, 5% more on a kill, up to 3 foes a blow), fading at 5% a second after 3 s without a blow, and cleared by any blow that takes his HP.
- The HP bar's debt segment with its repay, fade and forfeit looks, and a tick at 35%, shown only for him (`src/ui/debtBar.ts`).
- The axe string Hew, Reave and Cleave, the same every time, and Hatchet Drop in the air.
- Frenzy on K (12% HP, 4 s), Hunt and Headlong on L, War Cry (8% HP) and Red Crescent (10% HP) on I, and Berserk on U with Deathless and Last Cleave.
- 20 skills with evolutions and code-drawn icons, Brannoc's Trial and On the Edge (achievements go from 33 worth 2,470 coins to 35 worth 2,570), the tracked stat `bloodiedKills`, his keys on the Controls screen, his touch buttons with an HP cost rim on the Ability button, per-seat co-op memory, and Aftershock on every Hunt and Headlong landing.
- Shared code: the optional `hpFloor` kit hook in `hurtHero`, the `landed` kit hook in `dealDamage`, the `PAL.rage` ramp and `SPARKS.rage`, and the great axe in the shared weapon drawing; each does nothing for other heroes.
- Every hero's ability tabs now join one row that wraps on the right, so a lone tab never takes a row of its own, and on a landscape phone the tabs keep clear of the pause button.
- Taken out after QA's third round: Hunt pulling up out of a winding mini boss's or boss's swing (3e35bb3), since it never moved him out of reach, and a measured HUD placement (a2485ad), since it moved the HUD over the whole stage on 360 by 640 phones for seven heroes.

## QA verdict

The last QA round (qa-4, on f1b3a23) passed every check it ran.
Typecheck, lint and build pass.
The smoke check passes for all ten heroes with 154 checks, no failures and no page errors.
On the mash bot from wave 1 and the skilled bot from wave 20, the other nine heroes match the baseline exactly.
Brannoc falls on wave 5 with 14 kills on the mash bot, within two waves of every other hero, and at the wave 20 start he falls in 24.6 s with 0 kills like every hero.
A save from 0.9.0 loads intact, Brannoc shows locked at 2450 coins, and buying him takes 2450 coins.
The axe string, the Bloodied double Cleave, Fury, Frenzy and the debt, Hunt and Headlong, War Cry and Red Crescent, Berserk with Deathless and Last Cleave, the evolution through the dev start, the touch controls, co-op seat memory, the menus, the art and the docs all passed.
HUD placement across 10 window sizes and 14 hero setups showed no overlaps and nothing off-screen.
QA noted five minor issues, which ship as they are.
At some desktop window sizes (640 by 600, 700 by 700) the HUD sits partly above the screen, and at 600 by 540 the first tab row touches the pause button; this is also in 0.9.0.
A Hunt into a mini boss or boss that is winding up still gets him hit and clears his debt, though the chop lands, and the design note calls it a trade.
In real fights a Hew at 35% HP measured about 1.25x to 1.33x a Hew at full HP, below Fury's 1.39x, because shared hit bonuses shift from swing to swing.
The cost rim on the touch Ability button is accurate but small at phone size.
The evolved summons and Haemorrhage hit hard, and Carrion Storm's raven cloud wins back no debt.
