# 0.5.0 Long Chain: decision memo

Tonight is a big patch under the hero sprint, so the headline is a new hero, the fifth of ten.
Two pieces of the owner's day work ship beside it as extra highlights and need no plan from the studio.
One is hero skills, which replace relics with 20 skills per hero, each with eight levels and an evolution.
The other is the growth rework: charms, chests, Flow, streaming waves, damage by source and the Codex.

I chose Orrin, the chain dancer, a tether fighter with a brass hook on a long chain.
K throws the hook into a warp crystal, under a platform or into the air itself, and he swings under it as a real pendulum.
He pumps the swing with the arrow keys, reels in to speed it up, and lets go to fling himself across the stage.
His chain lash hits hardest at the tip, so he wants foes at the chain's length, never in his face.
His second bar, Momentum, fills while he flies fast and makes every blow hit harder, and it drains when he stands still.

He fills the gap the roster has left.
Kael teleports, Ysra vaults, Sable hops and Hulda charges, and none of them keeps speed through the air.
Orrin has no run, no dash, no block and no armour.
His defence is staying above and around foes, a Slip when he lets go just before a blow lands, and a spinning chain that breaks projectiles.
His reach sits between Ysra's glaive and Sable's crossbow, and his blows are arcs and full circles instead of pokes, bolts or grabs.

The tether fighter is first in the roadmap's suggested order, and the stage already supports it.
The three warp crystals and three platforms give him swing points, and the air bite means he can always swing on open floor.
The engine already has a verlet chain class for scarves that can draw his idle chain.
That makes him the hero most likely to ship complete at the charter's quality bar tonight.

I weighed the time or gravity mage next and kept it for tomorrow.
The summoner waits until moving foes safely is proven, and tonight's skills already give every hero summons.
The shadow assassin needs a foe awareness rule in shared code first.
The berserker would only change the resource, and the trapper and shield thrower sit too close to Sable.

I left balance out of tonight's plan on purpose.
The baseline shows every hero falling to the Rift Warden in about 21 to 24 seconds with no kills at wave 20.
The day work changed growth, cards and foe HP all at once, so I want to check that the wave 20 bot start matches a real run before tuning anything.
That moves to the roadmap with the new numbers.
The smoke check passed for every hero with no page errors, and there is no player feedback waiting, so the developer's whole night goes to Orrin.

Orrin costs 550 coins, comes with his trial and a Full Swing achievement, and gets a real benefit from every shop item.
His design note will say how he differs from each of the four heroes already in the game.

## What shipped

- Orrin, the chain dancer (studio, new-hero headline): 550 coins, 95 HP, move 1.05, a three-blow chain string (Lash, Rising Arc, Full Circle) with an 18 px tip band, the Chain Knee and the Hanging Arc.
- Orrin's hook and pendulum swing: crystal, platform, air bite (1.2 s, two per airtime) and zip to a foe, with pumping, reeling, the fling, swing kicks, the Slip, Backline and a ledge pull onto low platforms.
- Momentum, his MO bar: up to +40% on every hit at full, filled by speed in the air, drained by standing still and by hurts.
- Whirlwall and Anchor Pin on I, Grand Pendulum as his special, and 20 skills with evolutions and code-drawn icons, Maelstrom among them.
- Orrin's Trial and Full Swing achievements, his keys on the Controls screen, his touch buttons, and per-seat co-op memory.
- Deviations from the plan, recorded in docs/orrin-design.md: tip hits curl round guards, an air hook hangs him while it flies, a hook stopped short by the stage edge refunds its cost, low platforms give a ledge pull, Anchor Pin takes only foes on a floor, Perpetual Motion level 4 holds Momentum at 30 or more, and Perpetual Motion evolves into Endless Motion with Brass Kite.
- README fixes for Kael's and Ysra's starting kits and Ysra's two ultimates, and Hulda's Controls text naming her three blows (a8781fe).
- Hero skills (day work 8f3fa9f, new-system headline): the skill tree replaced by 20 skills per hero with eight levels and an evolution each, fixed combo strings, full base kits from the start, and a special on U for every hero.
- Growth rework (day work 02d161e, new-system headline): charms, chests from elites, mini bosses and the Warden, Flow, streaming waves from 3 to 62 foes, elites every third wave from wave 3, compounding hero power to about level 84, damage by source on the result screen, the Codex with hidden unlocks, shop pick tools, and the larger kill achievements.

## QA verdict

The final QA round (qa-2) passed.
Typecheck, lint and build pass, with only the over 700 kB chunk warning the base commit also has.
The smoke check passes for Kael, Ysra, Sable, Hulda and Orrin with no page errors.
The mash, skilled and wave-20 bots for the other four heroes match the baseline, and mash Orrin falls on wave 5 with 18 kills.
Orrin's skilled bot reaches the same waves as the previous build from start waves 2, 3, 4 and 6; the fresh-start drop to wave 3 is divergence in one deterministic run.
Anchor Pin now holds pinned thornbacks in place and skips foes high in the air, and its stake reads in front of the foe.
Saves from v0.4.0 and the base commit load with bank, shop levels, unlocks, options and hero choice intact.
QA noted one minor issue: a moth flying level with a platform can still be pinned to the platform's edge.
The developer noted that the skilled bot never uses Orrin's hook, and that Twin Chain's shade and Pendulum Kick's afterimage have not been seen in real play.
