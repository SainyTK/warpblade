# 0.13.1 Wave 5 and the Prism Knight: director's memo

Tonight is a small patch, with no day work, no new feedback and no commits since 0.13.0.
The smoke check passed for all nine heroes with no page errors, so nothing is broken in a way that blocks play.

The biggest measured problem is still wave 5.
On the baseline, the skilled bot clears Oakheart with only one hero of nine, Brynn, and the mash bot clears it with none.
Every other skilled run loses between 87 and 126 HP in that wave.
Oakheart's HP has already come down twice, from 400 to 240, and the failed runs leave it a few hundred HP short of death, so a third HP cut would mostly shorten a fight that is already close.
The roadmap named its front guard as the next thing to try once 0.13.1 allowed another Oakheart change.
So from tonight Oakheart drops its guard when it falls under half HP, the same moment it enrages and starts attacking faster.
A leaf burst and a shake mark the moment, so players can see the bark split and know to press in.
The first half still asks for back hits and heavy blows; the second half becomes a race.
This also helps the Prism Knight, whose thrusts were often blocked on the very boss that opens the job.
We aim for three to six of nine skilled clears, and we will rerun the bots to check.

The other three changes close the known issues 0.13.0 shipped with.
Crystal Nova's level prism beams flew over thornbacks and mires because they only struck a foe whose middle sat near the line.
They now strike any foe whose body they cross, with the same look and numbers.
The co-op lobby showed the Prism Knight's coat while the room plays the Warp Knight; it now shows the hero's own job in a room and puts the chosen job back when you leave.
The Prism Knight's crystal pauldron gets a dark violet outline and a little more width, so it reads on the pale coat.

We left the Rift Warden alone even though every wave 20 bot falls within half a minute.
Those bots start at level 14, about twenty levels below a real run at that point, so tuning from them would make the Warden too soft for real players.
The Bog Hulk at wave 10 waits until more bots reach it after the Oakheart change.
HUD and backdrop polish and the skill band work stay on the roadmap.

While reading the start code we found that the bots run the classic 30-wave arena, not the side-scrolling adventure, because their playtest flag skips it.
We will ask the owner whether the bots should play the adventure, and QA will check Oakheart's adventure room by hand tonight.

## What shipped

- Oakheart drops its front guard once it falls under half HP: a new optional `guarding` hook on `EnemyKind`, checked first in `guards()`, and set on Oakheart's kind from the shared `OAK_ENRAGE = 0.5`.
- At that moment 10 leaf shards burst from its crown with `shake(0.2, 2)` and `sfx.block()`, once per Oakheart.
- Oakheart's base HP went from 240 to 200, so 1,153 to 961 at wave 5 (`ENEMIES.oakheart.hp`).
- Its branch sweep went from 18 to 14 base damage, so 21.6 to 16.8 at wave 5 (`OAK_HITS.sweep`).
- Its rest between attacks above half HP is 1.25 times as long (`OAK_CALM_REST`), and its enraged rest stays at 0.65.
- Crystal Nova's prism beams test the foe's hit box with the new shared `segmentToBox` in `src/utils/segmentToBox.ts`, so the level beams strike grounded thornbacks and mires.
- In a co-op room Kael behind the menus wears the Warp Knight, and leaving the room puts the solo job back, through one listener on the room store (`src/ui/menu/bindRoomLook.ts`).
- The Prism Knight's pauldron plates have a one-pixel `PAL.prism[1]` rim and are 0.6 px wider each (3.2, 3.8 and 4.4).
- `docs/combo-design.md` and `docs/heroes/kael.md` describe the guard hook, the beam hit test, the pauldron rim and the co-op look.

The developer changed the plan once, after QA's first two rounds failed it.
The guard drop alone left the skilled bot at 2 of 9 wave 5 clears, because bots fell in Oakheart's first half before the guard drop could matter.
A version with the guard dropping at three quarters of its HP made the bots worse and was reverted.
The plan had locked Oakheart's HP, hits and cooldowns, but the developer added the HP, sweep and calm rest changes above, each within the charter's 25 percent limit.
Of the variants measured, the longer rest alone gave 2 of 9, with the HP cut 3 of 9, with the sweep cut 2 of 9, and all three together 4 of 9, which is what shipped.
Oakheart was last changed in 0.12.1, three patches ago.

Vesper's skilled run ends in a timeout at wave 7 because of the bot's known ledge stall.

## QA verdict

QA passed the patch on its third round (qa-2).
The first two rounds failed it on one major issue: the guard drop alone did not get more bots past wave 5.
Typecheck, lint and build pass, with only the existing Vite chunk-size warning.
The smoke check passes 139 checks for all nine heroes with no page errors.
QA's own bot runs match the developer's run for run, inside the target on both bots.
In frontal fights at wave 5, level 6, Oakheart blocked nothing after half HP and showed the leaf burst, and in the adventure's first section the burst also shows at the crossing.
A Prism Knight's Crystal Nova struck a grounded thornback and a grounded mire and lodged a shard on each.
Hosting a room and joining by invite both show the Warp Knight look, leaving puts the Prism Knight back, and the saved job is unchanged.
HP lost in waves 1 to 4 matches the baseline for every hero on both bots, menus open with no errors, and a save from 0.13.0 loads with its coins and purchases.

QA left five minor issues.
Wave 5 may now be easy for a hero who arrives with full HP: 8 of 9 heroes started fresh at wave 5 beat it, and the full-run number stays in the target only because of HP lost in waves 1 to 4.
The patch cuts Oakheart's HP and sweep, which the plan had considered and rejected, so the notes and roadmap describe all three stat changes with the guard drop.
The leaf burst reads weakly under the hit flash and damage numbers, and Oakheart looks the same afterwards.
The skilled bot's Vesper run still stalls on a ledge at wave 6 or 7 until the time limit.
Vite's large-chunk warning remains on build.
