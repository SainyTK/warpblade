# 0.14.1 decision memo: Fair randomness and fixes

Tonight is a small patch with no day work and no new feedback, so it starts from bugs and QA's open items.

## What we chose

The main change splits the game's randomness in two.
Effects like sparks, mud, embers and screen shake now draw from their own random generator, and the one that drives foes, drops and picks is left to play alone.
QA has reported since 0.13.2 that adding a single burst of leaves to Oakheart changed how every later fight in a bot run played out, which made our before and after numbers noisy for two patches.
While checking the code tonight we found a second, worse coupling.
The screen shake drew from the play generator once per painted frame, while the game itself steps at a fixed rate.
That meant a 120 Hz screen or a dropped frame could change what a foe did next.
It also broke a rule our own docs already state: drawing must never read the game's random stream.
After this patch, effects look and move as before, and adding or removing one can no longer change a fight.

The second change makes the Bog Rush's floor mark readable on phones.
The dashes are twice as long, get a dark outline against the bog floor and start brighter.
The end post is twice as tall, and the mark flashes bright in the last quarter second before the Hulk launches.
This was the known issue we shipped with 0.14.0.

The third change keeps the skill count badges inside their own slots in the compact HUD, so a full build in a narrow window no longer reads as overlapping icons.

## What we held back

No balance changes tonight, on purpose.
Moving effects to their own generator shifts every bot run once, and changing a number at the same time would leave us unable to tell which change did what.
The Bog Hulk is the obvious next target, since no bot clears wave 10 yet, but it waits for the clean numbers this patch produces.
Wave 5 is already inside its target band, with 4 of 9 skilled runs and 2 of 9 mash runs clearing it.
The wave 20 numbers still come from a bot that starts at level 14, far below a real run, so they do not tell us anything about the Rift Warden yet.
Oakheart's thin split and the larger HUD layouts stay on the roadmap.

## What comes next

QA's numbers after this patch become the new baseline.
The next small patch tunes the Bog Hulk from them, starting with the Rush.
The next big patch is due on 27 November and will rotate away from a new boss.

## What shipped

- A second seeded generator, `fxRng` (`FX_SEED = 20261125` in `src/constants/stage.ts`), beside the play generator `rng` in `src/game/state.ts`.
- `Particles` is built with `fxRng`, so its own per-spawn draws and the flames a burning chunk sheds come from it.
- `renderScene` draws the screen shake from `fxRng`, so the paint rate no longer moves the play stream.
- About 560 cosmetic draws across `src/game/`, the hero folders and `src/scene/eras/twilight.ts` moved to `fxRng`: spawn arguments, chance gates around cosmetic spawns, dust, afterimages, damage number jitter, impact rays, slash and bolt shapes and other fields only `draw()` reads.
- Everything play reads stays on `rng`: AI choices, cooldowns, spawn positions, foe seeds that steer motion, procs, crits, drops, pickups, coins, XP, chests, cards and every burning chunk (`PK.BRAND`) argument.
- Vesper's Dusk Current no longer draws from `rng` inside its `draw()`.
- A burning chunk ignores the 1,800 particle cap, so a full screen of sparks can no longer stop a blaze from lighting.
- A Biome `noRestrictedImports` rule forbids importing `rng` from `game/state` anywhere under `src/scene/`, and `docs/graphics.md` and `docs/hero-module.md` name `fxRng` for picture-only draws and `rng` for play.
- The Bog Rush path mark (`rushMark.ts`): dashes 4 px every 8 px (was 2 every 6) in three rows with a `veinDeep` outline, strength from 0.55 (was 0.3) to 0.9, an end post 8 px tall and 2 px wide (was 4 by 1) with a 3 px cap and an outline column, end glow radius 12 (was 8), and a `STORM_VOLT[4]` flash in the last 0.25 s of the wind-up.
- Compact HUD badges: under a 295 px tray the badge sits at right 0, bottom 0 (was -3 px each), and under 273 px it is a 6 by 6 px box with no padding.

Plan deviation from the developer report.
The plan asked for 6 or 7 px badge text under 273 px.
The HUD's pixel font, Silkscreen, blurred its digits at both sizes at 1x density, so the badges use its native 8 px in the 6 px box.

The developer report says 7 commits; the branch had 6 when QA checked it, and the media commit made 7.

## Runtime dependencies

None were added.
The patch does not change `package.json`.

## Balance

No balance number changed.
Every bot result moved once, because each fight now draws different random values when effects no longer share the play stream.

| Bot, one run per hero from wave 1 | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skilled, furthest wave (Kael, Cerise, Aldric, Vesper, Saya, Yun, Brynn, Zafir, Calder) | 5, 5, 6, 10, 5, 5, 7, 10, 5 | 3, 5, 8, 5, 5, 4, 5, 5, 8 |
| Skilled, past wave 5 | 4 of 9 | 2 of 9 (Aldric, Calder) |
| Mash, furthest wave | 5, 4, 5, 5, 5, 5, 8, 10, 5 | 4, 4, 3, 5, 4, 5, 6, 8, 5 |
| Mash, past wave 5 | 2 of 9 | 2 of 9 |

At wave 10, level 12, every hero still falls to the Bog Hulk in both bots, with 4 to 7 kills in the skilled runs.
Kael's mash run there fell at 34.8 s with 6 kills, against 45.4 s with 7 kills before.
At wave 20, level 14, every hero falls in 22.9 to 32.2 s with 0 to 2 kills (before: 21.6 to 31.6 s with 0 to 3).
Both skilled runs with `--runs 2` are identical for all nine heroes.
QA reran the skilled bot with two other play seeds: the patch averaged 5.78 and 5.89 furthest waves against 5.44 and 5.00 on the base, so the wave 5 drop with the default seed is seed noise, not a harder game.
It still puts the wave 5 skilled clears under the 3 to 6 target, so the wave 5 item is open again.

## QA verdict

QA passed the patch on its first round (qa-0).
Typecheck, lint and build pass, with only the known Vite chunk-size warning.
The smoke check passes 139 checks for all nine heroes with no page errors.
With 0 or 3 extra paints per game frame, play traces are identical at the patch for all 13 runs checked and split after 2 s on the base.
A burning chunk still spawns with the list at 1,800, its blaze lights, and the spawn does not move the play stream.
The lint rule fails a named `rng` import under `src/scene/` at two folder depths and passes an `fxRng` import.
The Bog Rush mark at 390 by 844 and 1280 by 720 is clearly stronger than the base, and the last-call flash shows even under the hero's feet.
The compact HUD badges stay inside their slots at 560, 600 and 640 px wide, and other widths are unchanged.
Menus open without errors, a save from the base loads with its coins, shop items and options, and the docs and README match.

QA left five minor issues.
Under 273 px the level 1 badge renders soft from a half-pixel position.
The badge text is 8 px where the plan said 6 or 7 px, as the developer documented.
The lint rule does not catch a namespace import of `game/state` under `src/scene/`.
With the default seed only 2 of 9 skilled heroes get past wave 5, below the 3 to 6 target.
The developer report counts 7 commits where QA found 6, and the developer's scratch worktrees are still registered in the repo.
