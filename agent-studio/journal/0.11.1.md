# 0.11.1 Balance and fixes: director's memo

Tonight is a small patch with no day work, no new commits since 0.11.0 and no player feedback in the queue.
So the plan comes from the baseline playtest and the roadmap.

## What the baseline showed

All 18 bot runs, nine heroes on the mash and skilled bots, ended at wave 5 or earlier.
The kill counts show what happened.
Waves 1 to 4 bring 14 foes and wave 5 brings Oakheart with four moths, and no run killed more than 18.
Oakheart did not fall once.
On the roster of 14 November, five of the heroes who fought without swords reached waves 8 to 10 on the same bot.
The nine sword heroes all fight at melee range, which puts them in front of Oakheart's guard and inside its branch sweep.

The smoke check passed for every hero, with one line that stood out.
Saya was the only hero whose U press after an ability did nothing.

## What we chose

First, a bug fix for Saya.
Brushline and Moonrise last about 0.7 seconds, the game holds a press for only 0.15 seconds, and her kit had no rule for when other actions may cut in.
So an ultimate, warp, guard or dodge pressed early in either ability was simply lost.
She now works like Brynn and Zafir: shared actions may cut in once the dash or the draw has landed.
If that cut comes before the blade clicks home, the click plays at once, so her stored cuts always release.

Second, Oakheart gets easier in two ways, each within the charter's 25 percent limit.
Its HP drops by a fifth, from 1,922 to 1,538 at wave 5.
Its branch sweep, the attack it uses on anyone at sword reach, drops from 22 to 18 base damage.
The roots, the acorns and the guard stay, so the fight still asks you to dodge.
The aim is for at least half the bot runs to clear wave 5, and we report the before and after numbers whatever they show.

Third, Yun's Sword Screen gets easier to read.
Until now his guard was drawn in thin, faint hairlines that were hard to see at 1280 by 720.
The swords now draw at full strength over a soft jade plate of light, and a blocked blow flashes them white.

## What we did not choose

We did not tune the Rift Warden.
The wave 20 bot starts at level 14, which is where a real run sits after wave 10, and our growth model puts a run at level 35 by wave 20.
Those 25 second falls tell us about the bot's starting level, so we have asked the owner to change it.

We left the weak ultimates alone because the rooms that measured them hit an overkill ceiling.
The skill measuring room is still the next big balance job.
The HUD placement waits for a layout that also holds on phones.

The shop prices and how evenly the heroes reach waves both depend on how far runs get.
We will measure both again once the bots can get past Oakheart.

## What shipped

- Saya: shared actions (Warp strike, guard, dodge, abilities, ultimate) may cut Brushline once the dash stops (held Brushline once she is back), and Moonrise and the counter draw once their blow has struck.
- A cut before the click plays the click at once, so stored cuts always release, and Long Stroke's launch still applies.
- Through Brushline, Moonrise and the counter draw, a dash, warp, cast, wide cast or ultimate press waits past the 0.15 s buffer for the cut moment, through the new optional `holdsSharedPresses` hook on `HeroKit` (used only by Saya).
- The guard is left out of the waiting presses, so a tapped K never parries late; a K held through the move raises the guard at the cut moment.
- Saya can dodge in the air out of Moonrise's rise.
- Silent Moon, Still Water and the Brushline dash still cannot be cut.
- Oakheart's base HP goes from 400 to 320 (1,922 to 1,538 at wave 5).
- Oakheart's branch sweep goes from 22 to 18 base damage (26.4 to 21.6 at wave 5 on an unarmoured hero).
- Yun's Sword Screen draws its swords at full strength (body 0.95, glow 0.45, up from 0.75 and 0.28 times 0.85) over an 8 by 16 px oval plate with a JADE[4] core at 0.4, a pale JADE[5] rim at 0.8 on whole art pixels and a dark JADE[1] edge at 0.6 outside it, with no dither.
- The plan asked for a soft 12 by 18 px JADE[3] plate at 0.22, which was invisible on the sunlit stage, so the developer drew the outlined plate.
- A block on the Sword Screen flashes the swords white for 0.08 s and throws 3 JADE[4] and JADE[5] flecks toward the attacker.

The Oakheart change is a partial step and did not reach the plan's target.
On the ordinary bots, 0 of 18 runs clear wave 5 after the change, the same as before, and 17 of 18 runs match the baseline exactly.
Started at wave 5 and level 6 on the skilled bot, 2 of 9 heroes clear it (Brynn and Zafir), against 0 of 9 before, and every hero leaves Oakheart with less HP when they fall.
Heroes enter wave 5 already worn down, and the root line, the moths and the acorns kill about as often as the sweep.
As the plan's fallback asked, the values stay at 320 and 18 tonight.
A later patch should look at the root line, the moth pressure in wave 5, or the HP heroes carry into the mini boss.

## Runtime dependencies

None were added.
The patch does not change `package.json`.

## QA verdict

QA passed the patch on its fourth round, on commit 0c9dd46, after a failed first round and two passing rounds that each sent a Saya fix back.
Typecheck, lint and build pass, with only the large-chunk build warning that was already there.
The smoke check passes for all nine heroes with no page errors, and Saya's line now reads "KeyU: line to moon".
In the browser, U early or late in Brushline and Moonrise goes to Silent Moon with the stored cut released, L out of Brushline dodges with the click, a tapped K no longer fires a late guard, and a held K guards at the cut moment.
Yun's Sword Screen reads clearly at 1280 by 720 and 390 by 844 with 0 and 4 swords, and its art uses only the JADE ramp and WHITE.
Menus open with no errors and a save from the base build loads with its coins and purchases.
The one failed check is the Oakheart target, which QA lists as a minor issue covered by the plan's fallback.
The other minor issues: a K held through Moonrise raises the guard in the air and she falls on release, which is the shared air-guard rule for every hero, and Yun's plate differs from the plan as described above.
