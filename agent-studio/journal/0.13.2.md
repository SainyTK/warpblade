# 0.13.2 Oakheart's split bark and fixes: director's memo

Tonight is a small patch with no feedback to answer and no day work, so it works through the bugs QA left open.

The first job is the skilled bot baseline.
It closed its page partway through and left no runs, while the mash, wave 20 and smoke runs on the same code passed.
The skilled bot passed on 0.13.0, so the developer checks first that 0.13.1 did not bring in a crash, one hero at a time.
If the game is at fault it gets fixed tonight; if the harness is, the owner gets a note saying which run was going.

The main change is Oakheart's split bark.
Since 0.13.1 Oakheart drops its guard under half HP, and that moment decides the wave 5 fight.
QA found the leaf burst easy to miss under the hit flash, and the tree looked the same afterwards.
Now the burst is bigger, with leaves, bark chips and violet sparks, and the trunk keeps a glowing violet split for the rest of the fight.
The split also flares with each wind-up, and the tree loses some moss and an acorn, so you can see the change from across the stage.
Its HP, damage and timings stay the same.

Two older visual bugs close as well.
The backdrop beside the stage mirrored the stage's sun, so at some window sizes the day sky had two suns, and it showed a lighter band at the stage's top line.
The compact HUD on small desktop windows wrapped a full build's charms onto a second row; they now stay on one line.

I left balance alone tonight.
The skilled bot clears wave 5 in 4 of 9 runs, inside the target, and Oakheart cannot be cut again this soon under the charter.
The heroes who still fall at wave 5 lose little HP before it, so easing waves 1 to 4 would not help them much.
The Rift Warden numbers still come from bots at level 14, far below a real run, so they wait on the owner.
Splitting the game's large build chunk properly means loading heroes on demand, which is too big for one night.

The next big patch is due tomorrow and should rotate away from new systems.

## What shipped

- The skilled baseline failure came from the studio harness, not the game.
  The baseline step in `agent-studio/src/steps/checks.ts` caps each bot command at 40 minutes, and the skilled command was killed at that mark while Vesper's run was going.
  On the base code her run reaches wave 7, stands on a ledge (x 228, y 114) above a Mire Toad and waits out the 30 game-minute limit, 2342 s of wall time.
  The page stayed responsive with steady memory near 500 MB, so there was no game change.
  The developer's notes ask the owner to keep a skilled report's finished runs when one run fails, and to cap a stalled run so it ends as a timeout inside the 40 minutes.
- Under half HP (`e.hp < e.max * OAK_ENRAGE`), `drawOakheart` draws a jagged split from y -28 to y -16: a `DC.barkDeep` gap with a `glowCol` core, `DC.crackHot` at two kinks, a `B[4]` and a `B[0]` lip and two `DC.crack` branches.
  The middle acorn and the two shoulder moss dots are left out while split; the moss dots sit under the crown, so only the acorn change shows.
- `splitBark` now spawns 16 leaf shards (was 10) for 0.7 to 1.2 s (was 0.5 to 0.9 s), 8 bark chips and 6 rift embers, with `shake(0.3, 3)` (was `shake(0.2, 2)`), still once per Oakheart.
- Each era can build a second plate without its sun, moons, rings, halos and the rain's sun haze (`buildBackground(def, k, arena, false)` and `PlatePaint.bodies`).
  The backdrop beside the stage fades from the real plate into the bodiless one over 32 art pixels, and the sky above the stage rises out of the stage's top rows over 24 px and into the era's top sky band over 72 px.
- The compact build strip no longer wraps and its tray is a CSS size container: slot gaps drop from 2 px to 0 below a 295 px tray, and slots go from 18 px to 14 px with 12 px icons below 273 px.
- `docs/graphics.md` describes the bodiless plate and the fade above the stage, and `docs/combo-design.md` describes the lasting split and the new burst.

Bot results moved for some heroes because the burst draws 30 more numbers from the gameplay random generator, so runs branch at Oakheart's half HP.
HP lost in waves 1 to 4 is identical, and wave 5 clears stay at 4 of 9.

## QA verdict

QA passed the patch on its first round (qa-0).
Typecheck, lint and build pass, with only the known Vite chunk-size warning.
The smoke check passes 139 checks for all nine heroes with no page errors.
QA confirmed from the harness log and code that the 40-minute cap closed the skilled baseline's page, not the game.
The split, the burst numbers and the shake match the plan, and the clip and stills at 1280 by 720 show the crack, its flare and the lost acorn.
Base and patch stills at four window sizes across waves 1 to 30 show one sun, moon or eclipse in every era and no band at the stage's top line.
Across a grid of 5,000 compact window sizes with a full build, the patch has no strip with more than one row, against 143 on the base.
QA's skilled runs match the developer's after-run figures exactly, and the wave 20 runs match the baseline.
Menus open with no errors, and a save from 0.13.1 loads with its coins and purchases.

QA left five minor issues.
Small, short windows about 524 to 676 px wide now get the compact HUD above the stage where they had the HUD inside it, which breaks the plan's rule that the HUD's place must not change.
In the narrowest compact windows the 14 px slots and 12 px icons are hard to read under the count badges, and at 640 px wide the slots touch and the badges overlap.
The split is thin at game scale and sits close to the rift veins, and removing the shoulder moss shows nothing.
The burst's cosmetic particles draw from the gameplay random generator, which makes before and after bot comparisons noisy.
At pixel scale 1 in small windows the stage's dithered sky shows stripes, a known issue from before this patch.
