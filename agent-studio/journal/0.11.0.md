# 0.11.0 Nine Blades: decision memo

The owner asked for this patch on 15 November 2026, outside the nightly studio, in these words: "Only Kael is fun to play and other weapon other than sword doesn't align well with the game story. I need characters that hold sword only. And the design of all characters should be like characters from JRPG games (final fantasy as inspiration)."
So the nine heroes who carried other weapons leave the game, eight sword heroes take their places, and Kael gets a look that matches theirs.

Kael set the bar for every new hero.
His presses answer at once, his warp closes any gap, an ability can cut into his string, a parry opens a counter, and Keen timing rewards rhythm over mashing.
Each new hero has its own version of each of those, and one thing no other hero does: Cerise chains ripostes on one thin line, Aldric attacks from behind a raised shield, Vesper speeds up with every blow and lives in the air, Saya's cuts land late and all at once at the sheathe, Yun's spirit swords repeat his blows at range, Brynn swings through hits and clears a crowd with one blow, Zafir walks while he spins and cuts both sides, and Calder fires a round into the wound and counts his chamber.
Nothing the owner removed from the old heroes came back: no long recharges, no travel moves that carry the hero far and lock out the kit, no attacks the game picks on its own and no attacks that turn the hero around.

## How it was built

A foundation came first.
It removed the nine heroes and every module only they used, and turned each hero into a self-contained module under `src/heroes/<id>/` behind one roster, with Kael as the worked example (`docs/hero-module.md`).
It also fixed the guard against a touching foe, and registered the eight new heroes as stand-ins that played as Kael, so every hero worktree started from a game that ran.

Then nine agents worked in parallel, one per hero: eight built a new hero and one gave Kael his new look.
Each owned only its own hero folder and its two notes, `docs/heroes/<id>.md` and `docs/skills/<id>.md`, and lint kept every hero out of the others' folders.
Each hero agent built a look on the shared rig, every clip, the string, the meter, the guard and parry, the warp extra, two abilities with wide forms, the ultimate, 20 skills with evolutions and icons, its achievements, touch art and the Controls text, and checked its hero with the smoke check and both bots against Kael on the same commit.
The branches merged into the roster branch, and a last commit removed the stand-in flag, renamed Aldric's guard achievement to Bulwark so it no longer shares Cerise's Unbroken Line, and let Kael's held guard reach its block and parry keys.
Final QA and polish ran on the roster branch at the same time as these notes.

The studio's rules change with the roster.
The hero sprint is over, the roster is set at nine sword heroes in the charter, and a new hero comes only when the owner asks for one.

## What shipped

- Eight new heroes, in hero screen order and price: Cerise (free, rapier, Points and the riposte chain), Aldric (300, sword and shield, Valor and Radiance), Vesper (400, long and short sword, Tempo), Saya (550, katana, stored cuts and Focus), Yun (750, jian, spirit swords), Brynn (1000, greatsword, armour, Heft and the fissure), Zafir (1350, scimitar, Whirl, the sand ring and wounds) and Calder (1800, gun sword, Triggers and a six-round chamber).
- Kael's new look: silver spiked hair with a tied tail, a midnight blue long coat, a single pauldron, a split red scarf and a broader crystal longsword, with no change to play; his hero screen title is now "The warp knight".
- Ysra, Sable, Hulda, Orrin, Mirel, Wenna, Tallis, Jory and Brannoc removed, with their skills, evolutions, achievements and the shared code only they used.
- A one-time refund of the 0.10.0 price of every removed hero a save had bought (`REMOVED_HERO_PRICES`), shown on the start menu until the next run.
- The guard: one block per touch per foe for 0.8 s, a broken guard that stays down until K is pressed again, and no cost for a blow that could not have hurt the hero.
- Achievements go from 35 worth 2,570 coins to 40 worth 2,900, with a trial for every hero and 12 achievements of the new heroes' own.
- The paid heroes together cost 6,150 coins, down from 8,600.
- Day work since 0.10.0, shipped here: the shared moveset (J held warps, K guards and parries, L dodges, I casts with wide forms on I held, one ultimate per hero), the floating crystals and the hang removed, the run clock and records, and touch play on iPhone and iPad.
- The start menu footer and its notes read against the floor.

## Open items from the hero reports

- Balance is not settled for any new hero. Each agent built its own measuring room in a scratch folder, since the repo has none, and the rooms are noisy: single runs swing by 20 to 40 a second, and several skills read lower at level 8 than at level 1.
- Over their bands in those rooms: Cerise's Measured Touch on a crowd (745 a second against a cap of about 300), Calder's Point Blank on a crowd (320), Zafir's Deep Wounds and Sand Wraith. Brynn's base kit read about twice Kael's in her room, while the bots put her level with him.
- Under their bands: most of Yun's and Vesper's level 8 skills, Aldric's level 8 adds, Saya's Clean Sheathe and Ink Shade, Calder's Hair Trigger, Smoke Double, Tracer Warp and Speed Loader, and Zafir's Sirocco Timing, Glass Rain, Desert Thirst, Desert Hawk, Two-Faced Cut and Whirlwind Arrival.
- Measured after their last tuning step: none of Calder's last row changes, Zafir's Desert Noon, Court of Mirages, Desert Thirst, Desert Hawk and Sandstorm, and Saya's Silent Moon, Reverse Edge and Lingering Line.
- Skills the rooms never drove: parry, warp, air and Keen skills for most heroes (for example Cerise's Riposte Chain and Grace, Saya's Deep Breath and Calm Mind, Yun's Flowing Water, Brynn's Crater Leap and Shoulder Charge, Calder's Blast Jump).
- Ultimates per cast are short of their targets for several heroes (Vesper's Dusk Waltz read 1.2 to 2.3 times the base special against about 6 at level 8, and Brynn's evolved Crimson Fall 5.4 times against about 15), and Yun's one evolved Jade Rain run looked broken.
- Co-op was not played with any new hero; their state is registered per seat but untested in a room.
- The touch lines on the Controls screen were not seen on screen for most heroes, because the harness cannot emulate a coarse pointer, and Saya's in-run touch buttons were not seen.
- Shared UI at 844 by 390: the skill pick cards overflow the screen on the left, and the hero screen hides the blurb.
- Yun's ability tabs read "SW" as their cost, and his Sword Screen guard swords are hard to make out at 1280 by 720.
- Kael keeps the shared rest stance, and his longer sword's point sits about 3 pixels below the feet line at rest.
- The shared warp flash hides Aldric's shield-first arrival for a frame or two.
- Some of Vesper's skill icons are plain at 16 pixels (Crossing Storm, Dusk Gale, Dusk Shade).
- Cerise's string has five blows, where `docs/skill-design.md` asks two to four for new heroes; the roster brief asked for four or five.
- The README's story still calls Kael "the last warp knight", while his hero screen title is now "The warp knight".
