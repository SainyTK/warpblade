# 0.15.0 Hold the Crystal: decision memo

Tonight's patch is the owner's day work.
Four headlines landed on main since 0.14.2: the crystal defense, the monster ladder, the shared skill light cap with mastery marks, and the Rift campaign with Resonance growth.
A day with a headline makes the night a big patch with that work as its headline, so the studio ships it and builds nothing large of its own.

I named the crystal defense the headline category, new-system, because it changes what a run is.
A run is now one stage held for 10 minutes at the Warp crystal, with foes from four sides, big foes on the clock, timed revives and cards on level up.
The other three pieces ship under it, and the notes cover each one.

The charter puts broken things first, so I read the day work for bugs a player would meet.
The baseline smoke check passed for all nine heroes with no page errors, and the triage list is empty.
Three leftovers from the change are worth fixing tonight.

The first is the save.
The Armory replaced the Shop, the meta save key in code is now `warpblade-meta-v2`, and an older doc still names a v1 save.
If 0.14.2 players load this build and find their coins and purchases gone, that breaks the charter's save rule.
The developer reproduces it with both builds in one browser first, and migrates the old save only if something is lost.

The second is the touch Controls screen.
It still tells phone players to tap Talk by a mentor, and the defense has neither a mentor nor a Talk button.

The third is the README, whose gameplay GIF and two stills show Rift Surges, which no longer exist.
New captures of the defense replace them.

I weighed tuning the boss fights, since challenge was the weakest dimension in the last scored build.
Those readings predate the defense, the new Fun definition has no readings yet, and the charter asks for numbers before balance.
Tonight's analyst seeds that baseline, and 0.15.1 can tune from it.

I also weighed edge marks for the Sky and Below sides and removing the mentor's face from the level-up cards.
Both are calls on a system the owner built yesterday, so they go to the roadmap for the owner.

The Fun target is clarity.
Game logic is unchanged apart from the save check, so every measure should read the same on the base and the patch, and the clarity judges should find the Controls screen and README accurate.

## What shipped

Day work, built by the owner and merged to main since 0.14.2:

- The crystal defense (e06d4913): each stage is a 10-minute defense of the Warp crystal (1200 HP, +15 percent per tier) against fronts that open at 0:00, 1:30, 3:30 and 6:00, an elite at 1:35, the mini boss at 4:15 and the boss at 7:30, revives after 3 x 1.5 per fall seconds up to 30, and cards 0.35 s after a level up that gives a point.
- The monster ladder (c6e80b7b): 72 new stage monsters on an eight-step ladder per stage with fixed HP, flinching steps 1 to 4, armored steps 5 to 8 and big foes, and screen shake only for heavy hits.
- The shared skill light cap, mastery marks and menu panel (6ee56ca1): no white bleach from skill light, element-tinted skill cores, Mastery pips in the build slots and HUD strip, tiered skill effects for every hero, and the start menu on a panel.
- The Rift campaign and Resonance (d14b0196): ten stages on the Rift Map with tiers, twists and Hyper, five elements with Fusions at rank 12 and finishing powers at rank 20, the Armory in place of the Shop, and the unlock table.
- Also since 0.14.2: the stage moveset for every hero (three-slash string, charged skill on held J, warp strike on U), Heart and Mana Shards with pacts, three-phase bosses with a stagger bar and enrage, the new result screen, sound and music with a Music volume, per-player co-op cameras, and new foe, boss and icon art.

Studio work tonight, on studio/patch-0.15.0:

- A 0.14.2 save under `warpblade-meta-v1` now carries over when no v2 save exists, and the old key is left untouched.
- Shop levels the Armory still sells are kept up to its level limit; levels over the limit, Head Start and Last Light are refunded at their 0.14.2 prices.
- The touch Controls screen lists a Leave row in place of the Talk row, and the keyboard list gains an Esc row with the same text.
- Esc on the level-up cards puts them away without also opening the pause menu.
- The 'Next from the ...' warning now shows 4 s before every new front opens, not only before swaps inside a front.
- The README gameplay GIF, the Veil Spires still and the late build still show the defense, and `docs/growth-design.md` no longer describes Rift Surges.

Plan deviations from the developer report.
The plan named only removed items for refunds; levels over the new Armory limits are refunded too, so no purchase is lost.
Acceptance check 3 failed on the base build because new fronts opened with no warning, so the developer made the smallest change in the owner's spawner that makes it pass.

## QA verdict

QA passed the patch on the first round (qa-0).
Typecheck, lint and build pass, with only the known chunk-size warning.
The smoke check passes for all nine heroes with no page errors.
A 0.14.2 save built and played through the real UI loaded on this patch with its bank, refunds at measured 0.14.2 prices, kept Armory levels, Aldric unlocked, achievements and options, and the v1 key untouched.
All 14 acceptance checks pass, including Crystal held at 0:00 with records saved and the next stages opened, and the defense at 390 by 844 with the touch controller.
All nine heroes held the crystal to 3:00 with the skilled bot, matching the base build within the small random shift the developer predicted.
The secret works.

QA left eight minor issues.
The stage name banner at run start is overwritten by 'Foes from the West' on the first tick.
The README late build still has no crystal in frame and its foes are washed pale by the per-hit flash.
A foe under constant procs late in a run stays near white from the per-hit flash.
The 5:56 warning 'Next from the West and East and Sky and Below' runs across the whole screen, and level-up banners overwrite side banners within a second.
The touch Switch button's icon reads as an empty dark circle.
The keyboard Controls list has two Esc rows, and the cards row also shows in co-op rooms, where wave picks have no Leave.
`docs/multiplayer.md` still says 'every rift surge'.
The v1 carry-over runs only when no v2 save exists, so a player who already played a deployed build with the day work keeps that v2 save and never gets the 0.14.2 bank back.

## Fun

The Fun score is 49.3 on the base build (4fc16c4a) and 49.3 on the patch (742ecece), under definition 3.
No dimension moved: control 80, challenge 46.4, progress 90, clarity 25, audiovisual 50, mastery 13, autonomy 84.4, variety 75 and immersion 50 on both builds.

Two readings moved inside a dimension without changing its score.

One R1 judge went from 4 to 3, so control's judges read 3, 4 and 3 on the base and 3, 3 and 3 on the patch, with the same median.

The plan targeted clarity (R4) to hold or rise one level.
It held at 2, scoring 25: the judges read 2, 3 and 2 on the base and 2, 2 and 2 on the patch.
Their evidence names screens tonight's patch does not touch: the result screen shows Best run as Not yet and 0 after an S clear, Damage by source is an empty header, Time reads 4:08 on a 10-minute defense, card tags contradict their text, bosses are missing from their own arrival stills, and the HP drain shows no source.
All three judges also wrote that every patch still was pixel-identical to the base and that no still showed the new 'Next from' banner, so the evidence job may not have filmed the patch build, and the clarity reading for tonight's fixes is unverified.
The plan expected the measures to read the same on both builds, and they did apart from M3.2, which the developer's note on the front warning's earlier random draw explains.

Tonight seeds the definition 3 baseline for 0.15.1.
The weakest readings are M2.4 (big foe fights take 8.1 times their designed kill time), M6.1 (-0.24, mashing removes more boss HP than skilled play), M2.3 (rho 0.061, difficulty barely climbs by stage tier) and R6 (mastery, 2).
