# 0.6.0 Borrowed Time: decision memo

Tonight is the third night of the hero sprint, so the headline is a new hero.
No feedback came in, no commits landed since 0.5.0, and the baseline smoke check and bot runs show no errors, so nothing broken jumps the queue.

I chose Mirel, the hour keeper, a time mage.
The roadmap named a time or gravity mage as next, and a control hero sits furthest from Orrin, who shipped last night as a fast aerial fighter.
Mirel bends time in three ways no hero has.
Every staff blow repeats itself half a second later as an echo, so her attacks land twice and she can leave a hit waiting for a foe that walks in.
Holding K opens a Stillfield where foes and their shots crawl, and her echoes hit at full power on foes inside it.
L rewinds her backwards along her own path and gives back part of the HP she lost, which is a defence built on undoing a mistake.
She spends Sand on those two moves, and Sand refills only while she waits, so her rhythm is to set up, strike, and let the hourglass fill again.

Against the roster she has no run, no dash, no block, no armour and no swing.
She is the most fragile hero and the most patient one, and her best fights happen next to a still crowd.

I weighed a gravity mage, a summoner, a berserker and a shadow assassin.
The gravity mage's lifts and slams sit close to Hulda's throws and Kael's Sky Dance pull.
The summoner is a strong next pick and moves up to next in the roadmap.
The berserker would be a second close-range bruiser beside Hulda.
The assassin needs a foe awareness rule first.

The Stillfield adds one small shared module so foes and the six kinds of foe shot can be slowed in an area.
Future time and control skills can reuse it.

Beside the hero, one small fix ships: Orrin's Anchor Pin no longer pins a flying moth to the edge of a platform, the known issue we listed in 0.5.0.

I did not tune the Rift Warden.
The wave-20 bot starts at level 14, while a real run now reaches about level 84 by the end, so those numbers do not reflect a real fight yet.
Orrin's low skilled bot result comes from the bot never using his hook, which is for the owner to address in the playtest folder.
I cut a spell that sends foes back along their own path, because it needs a position history for every foe; it waits in Later.

## What shipped

- Mirel, the hour keeper (studio, new-hero headline): 750 coins, 88 HP, 12 ability power, move 0.95, one 50 px jump, and a hover of up to 1.5 s at the top of a jump.
- Her staff string (Tick, Tock, Toll, and Pendant Strike in the air), with an echo of every blow 0.5 s later at 0.5x, or 1.0x on a foe in her field or stopped.
- The Stillfield on K (radius 44, foes at 0.2 to 0.6 pace, foe shots at 0.1, a snap on close) and Rewind on L (1.2 s of path, 40% of lost HP back), both spending Sand on the new SD bar.
- Stopped Clock and Second Hand on I, Hour of Stillness on U, and 20 skills with evolutions and code-drawn icons, Eternal Noon among them.
- Mirel's Trial and Hold the Hour achievements, her keys on the Controls screen, her touch buttons, per-seat co-op memory, and Aftershock on her Rewind and hover landings.
- Shared code: `src/game/timeFields.ts` slows foes and the six foe shots in an area, and the foe `stopped` status stops a foe and stores its damage; a stopped foe cannot guard.
- Stopped Clock aims at a foe level with her first (86813ad).
- Every hero stands in their own rest pose behind the title screen and the hero screen (a091f12, d029945), which keeps Mirel's staff ring out of the floor.
- Deviations from the plan are recorded in the As built section of docs/mirel-design.md, among them Patience evolving into Timeless with Past Self and Past Self's ghost striking her whole string.
- Orrin's Anchor Pin skips every foe kind that flies, and the known-issue line is gone from docs/orrin-design.md.

## QA verdict

The last QA round (qa-3, on 86813ad) failed on one check, the art finish.
On the hero screen, focusing Mirel's tile kept the previous hero's pose, so her clock ring was drawn inside the floor bricks.
Commit d029945 came after that round and fixes it: the hero screen now draws the hero in focus in that hero's own rest pose.
No QA round has run on d029945, and the run summary records the night as failed for that reason.
The developer re-checked d029945: typecheck, lint, build and the all-hero smoke run pass, with only the over 700 kB chunk warning the base commit also has.
Every other qa-3 check passed: save compatibility, the hero screen price and unlock, the hover, echoes, the Stillfield, Rewind, Stopped Clock, Hour of Stillness, skills and evolution, the Codex, achievements, Controls, touch at phone size, co-op and the Anchor Pin fix.
The five existing heroes match the baseline on both bots, and Mirel falls on wave 5 with both (mash 16 kills, skilled 14 kills).
QA noted three minor issues, which the developer kept as they are.
Without the Stillfield, early echoes miss often (9 of 29 hit in waves 1 to 3), which is how the kit is meant to work.
Holding W to aim the Stillfield up also makes her jump, because W is the shared jump; the field still opens at the height she aimed.
Neither playtest bot presses K for Mirel, so her bot numbers leave out the field until the owner adds a Mirel policy.
