# 0.8.0 Unseen: decision memo

Tonight is the fifth night of the hero sprint, so the headline is the eighth hero.
There is no day work, no feedback in triage, and no commits since 0.7.0.
The baseline is clean: the smoke check passes for all seven heroes with no page errors, and no bot run raised an error.

## What we chose

Tallis, the gloam knife, a shadow assassin.
He is first in the roadmap's suggested order for the last three heroes.
The engine already gives every foe a facing, a turn delay before it turns, a rule that foes only start a wind-up while facing the hero, and 1.5x damage for blows from behind.
So the assassin builds on rules players already meet with every hero.

He plays unlike anyone else in the roster.
His defence is being out of sight: a Veil on K that makes nearby minions lose him, and a shadow pool on L that glides under foes and hides him.
His resource, Gloam, refills while no foe faces him and from blows from behind, so where he stands decides how much he can do.
His string changes when he is behind a foe, and those blows deal 2.25 times as much.
He has the lowest HP and the shortest reach in the game, so standing in front of a crowd is a mistake.

The one new piece of shared code is a foe awareness rule.
A hidden hero is one foes cannot turn toward or wind up at, and a blind foe sees nobody.
With no hidden hero it changes nothing, so the other seven heroes play exactly as before.
The Rift Warden ignores it, and mini bosses only turn more slowly, so the Veil cannot trivialise the bosses.

## Alternatives

The rhythm fighter is just as distinct, but it needs a beat clock and sound sync on top of a full kit.
It is next in line.
The berserker sits close to Hulda's fantasy of walking into blows, and the shield thrower overlaps Sable's range and Ysra's block.
Either can be the tenth hero.

For his movement we looked at a pounce over foes and a dash behind them.
Ysra already vaults over foes and Kael already dashes through them.
The pool is slow, held and steerable, it hides him, and it ends in a rise that sets up an Ambush.

## What waits

The Rift Warden at wave 20 still kills every hero in about 18 to 28 seconds with no kills.
The roadmap asks us to check whether the wave-20 start matches a real run's level and build before tuning, so it stays the top small patch candidate.
Wenna's Flare cone starting behind her lantern is a small visual fix for a later small patch.

## Balance

The mash bot only walks and presses J.
Tallis must fall within two waves of the other heroes, who fall on waves 4 and 5.
If he falls early, his HP goes up first, then his front blows.

## What shipped

- Tallis, the gloam knife (studio, new-hero headline): 1350 coins, 90 HP, 11 attack, 8 ability power, 80 MP, 10% crit (the highest in the roster), move 1.08, one 58 px jump.
- HP went from the plan's 80 to 90 and Flick and Cross 15% stronger (Flick 0.805x, Cross 0.92x), both by the plan's fallback steps after QA's plain script; this leaves Sable (85) and Mirel (88) below him.
- The Veil on K: 20 Gloam to raise, 8 a second, a 1 s lock after it ends, 0.35 alpha with a rose outline, walk 15% faster, and an Ambush (a sure crit) on the first blow out of it.
- Gloam on the new GL bar: 100 max, 50 at the start, 5 a second while Unseen within 140 px, 6 per blow from behind and 12 more on a kill.
- Sink on L: 20 stamina to start and 40 a second for up to 1 s, a 115 px/s glide under ground foes, untouchable, a perfect dodge in the first 0.12 s, Rising Cut out of the pool, an Ambush window of 0.5 s after rising within 24 px behind a foe, and the air drop at 360 px/s.
- The front string (Flick, Cross, Heel Kick), the behind string (Backstab, Twist, Gut Slash) at 2.25x from behind with the back-open chevron, Drop Stab in the air, and a springing Rising Cut under hovering fliers.
- Every knife blow staggers by the shared default unless its row sets one, and a knife that lands on a winding minion cuts its wind-up short (61593a8).
- Smoke Knot and Hamstring on I, Gloaming on U, and 20 skills with evolutions and code-drawn icons, Eclipse among them.
- Night Hunter now evolves with Quiet Step and Phantom Walk with Night Leap, since the plan's partner Smoke Knot is a base ability; skills that had fewer than four new properties gained a small one each (recorded in docs/skills/tallis.md).
- Tallis's Trial and Knife in the Dark (achievements go from 28 worth 1,770 coins to 30 worth 1,870), the tracked stat `backKills`, his keys on the Controls screen, his touch buttons, per-seat co-op memory, and Aftershock on every rise out of the pool.
- Shared code: `src/game/enemies/awareness.ts` (`heroHidden`, `foeSees`), the optional kit hooks `hidden` and `heroRing`, the foe `blind` status, and the skin flag `scarfEnd`.
- Foes that cannot see the hero keep their facing, start no wind-up, walk on past, do no harm by touch and raise no guard against him; mini bosses turn twice as slowly toward a hidden hero, and the Rift Warden always sees him.
- In co-op, foes go for the nearest standing hero they can see, and for the nearest standing one when every hero is hidden.

## QA verdict

The last QA round (qa-3, on 4a6d22a) passed every check.
Typecheck, lint and build pass, with only the chunk size warning that predates the patch.
The smoke check passes 124 checks for all eight heroes with no page errors.
On the mash bot the seven other heroes match the baseline exactly, and Tallis falls on wave 4 with 11 kills, inside the plan's window of waves 3 to 7.
On QA's plain script Tallis, Kael and Hulda all end on wave 3, with 6, 9 and 7 kills.
The skilled bot has no Tallis policy and ends on wave 2 with 4 kills; from the wave 20 start he falls to the Warden in 18.7 s, inside the baseline's range.
A 0.7.0 save loads intact, and buying Tallis takes 1350 coins and unlocks him.
The Veil, the awareness rule, both strings, Sink, the abilities, Gloaming, the Warden, touch, Controls, achievements, the Codex, the evolution card, co-op and the art finish all passed.
QA noted three minor issues, which ship as they are.
A blocked Flick gives Tallis no pushback, so a guarding husk can walk into him and hurt him by touch before Heel Kick comes out.
Started from the far end of the chevron's range, the foe can walk out of Twist's reach during Backstab.
The skilled bot still has no Tallis policy, the chevron reads like a small heart at knife range, and the touch buttons carry icons with no text labels, as for every hero.
