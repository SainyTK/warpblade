# 0.3.0 decision memo

Tonight ships 0.3.0 as a big patch, and its headline is the owner's day work: co-op rooms for up to four players.
The commit 93ce4bf carries the new-system headline, so by the charter the day work sets the headline and the category.
The studio adds no changes of its own tonight.

What ships: one player hosts a room from the start menu, friends join with a five-letter code or an invite link, and everyone fights the same 30-wave run with their own hero.
Experience is shared so the room levels together, but each player keeps their own picks, shop upgrades and coins.
A fallen hero stays down until the wave is cleared, then stands with half HP, and the run ends only when everyone is down at once.
Foes grow in number and HP for each extra player.
This answers feedback #10 directly and gives players a new way to play the run they know, which serves pillar 4.

The day work also covers two more feedback items.
Feedback #8 asked for a warning at low HP, and the screen edge now glows red below 25 percent and beats below 10 percent, while both heroes sink into a wounded stance.
Feedback #9 said Ysra was too slow and fragile next to Kael, and her walk, vault, backstep, parry and Flare all got a survival pass.
The frame is now drawn at 640 by 360 with detailed heroes and redrawn foes in every era.

I weighed adding a Kael buff.
The baseline bots fall with Kael two waves before Ysra in skilled play, but players told us the opposite last week, and Ysra's changes are brand new.
I would rather measure both heroes after this patch and act in a small patch with before and after numbers.

I also weighed a Rift Warden damage cut.
At wave 20 both skilled bots fell in about half a minute, Ysra without a single kill.
That is a real problem, but the Warden's HP changed last patch and the foes were all redrawn today, so I keep one change at a time and put the Warden first for the next small patch.

Evolutions round two stays at the top of the roadmap for a later big patch, since evolutions were the last headline.

One note for the owner: co-op loads PeerJS and leans on the public PeerJS broker, and the charter says the game takes no new runtime dependencies.
I have flagged it in the roadmap notes for a decision.

## What shipped

- Co-op rooms for up to four players (feedback 10): host from the start menu, join by five-letter code or invite link, P1 to P4 seat colours with floor rings and tags, shared experience, own picks, shop levels and coins.
- Downed heroes rise with 50% HP at wave clear, and the run ends when every hero is down at once.
- Foe scaling per player past the first: +40% minions, +25% minion HP, +50% mini boss HP, +60% boss HP.
- The co-op status line under the pause button, which takes one row on upright phones (headline commit 93ce4bf).
- Low HP warning (feedback 8): a red edge glow below 25% HP (18 px, 1.8 s breath) and below 10% (28 px, 0.85 s beat), with a wounded idle for both heroes.
- Ysra's mobility and survival pass (feedback 9): move 0.93 to 1.1 (walk 62.7 to 74.2 px/s), vault recharge 1.2 to 0.95 s, vault untouchable to 0.1 s past the peak, vault out of a brace or backstep, backstep 30 to 36 px, 25 to 20 stamina and 0.12 to 0.15 s untouchable, 25 stamina back on a parry, and Flare heals 5% of max HP.
- The frame is drawn at 640 by 360, with detailed heroes and every era's foes redrawn.
- A fresh hero at the start of every run.
- Tonight's studio fixes: the Co-op screen fits upright phones, the host's focus moves to Start run when the first guest joins, the guest video opens at 2 Mbit/s, the status line takes one row on upright phones, Lodestone and Last Light shop texts name the hero, and the README and co-op notes were corrected.

## QA verdict

The final QA round (qa-2) passed.
Typecheck, lint, build and the smoke check for both heroes passed with no page errors.
Every co-op acceptance check passed: the Co-op screen and code input at 320 to 1280 px wide, host and guest lobbies, two heroes with rings and tags, guest input and HUD, the one-row waiting line at 375 by 667, downed and rising heroes, pause, leaving and host closing, and the unknown-code error.
Solo play shows no tag or ring and makes no network requests.
The low HP glow, the wounded stance and Ysra's walk, vaults and Flare heal matched the plan in the running game.
A save from v0.2.0 kept its coins, purchases and options.
Bot runs matched the baseline exactly.
QA noted three minor issues.
Ysra's hero card and the README still call her slower than Kael, which no longer holds.
The skilled Ysra bot from wave 20 lasts 34.9 s with 0 kills, against 41.6 s with 2 kills on v0.2.0, left for the Rift Warden small patch.
package.json still reads 0.2.0 until the finalize step bumps it.
