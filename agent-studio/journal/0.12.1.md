# 0.12.1 Balance and fixes: director's memo

Tonight is a small patch with no day work, no new commits since 0.12.0 and no player feedback in the queue.
The plan comes from the baseline bots, the 0.12.0 known issues and the roadmap.

## What the baseline showed

The smoke check passed for all nine heroes with no page errors.
On the skilled bot, eight of nine heroes fell in wave 5 with all 18 foes before Oakheart killed.
Several of them reached wave 5 almost unhurt: Zafir had lost 20 HP before it and Brynn 27, and both then lost their whole bar to Oakheart.
On the mash bot no hero cleared wave 5.
Only Vesper's skilled run got past it, to wave 10.
So the wave 5 mini boss still ends almost every run, two patches after we first eased it.

## What we chose

First, Oakheart gets a second, larger step.
The charter allows no second nerf to the same thing within three patches, and 0.12.1 is the third patch after the 0.11.1 nerf.
Its HP drops by a quarter, from 1,538 to 1,153 at wave 5, which is the largest step the charter allows.
Its root line drops from 20 to 16 base damage.
We chose the root line because after the sweep was eased in 0.11.1, the roots, moths and acorns were killing heroes about as often as the sweep.
A hero who keeps their distance to circle behind Oakheart's guard is exactly the player the roots punish.
The guard, the sweep, the acorns and every wind-up stay as they are, so the fight still asks you to read it.
We will report the bot numbers before and after, including how long each hero takes to bring Oakheart down.

Second, the Gloam Shepherd's Lantern Procession now looks the way it plays.
Its three rings overlapped so much that they read as one wide ring, which we listed as a known issue in 0.12.0.
Each ring is now smaller and they sit side by side, covering exactly the same floor as before, so the attack is no harder or easier.

Third, the HUD stays on screen in desktop windows such as 640 by 600 and 700 by 700, and stays clear of the pause button at 600 by 540.
This has been a known issue since 0.9.0.
The HUD now moves above the stage only when its measured height fits there.
The fix in 0.10.0 was taken out because it broke phones, so this time QA checks phone sizes as closely as desktop ones.

## What we did not choose

We left the Gloam Shepherd's HP, kneel and jump timing alone, since no bot has fought it at a level a real run would have.
We did not tune the Rift Warden for the same reason: the wave 20 bot starts at level 14, and we have asked the owner to raise it.
We kept Oakheart's guard, because circling behind a guarding foe is a read the game teaches from wave 3.
If wave 5 is still a wall after tonight, the guard is the next thing to look at.
Hero balance and the skill bands wait until runs get past wave 5, since every hero but one currently ends in the same place.

## What shipped

- Oakheart's base HP went from 320 to 240, so 1,538 to 1,153 at wave 5 (`ENEMIES.oakheart.hp`).
- Oakheart's root line went from 20 to 16 base damage, so 24 to 19.2 per spike at wave 5 (`OAK_HITS.root`).
- The Lantern Procession is three rings of 9 px radius with centres 19 px apart (`PROCESSION = { radius: 9, gap: 19, shrink: 5 }`), over the same 56 px band as before.
- `MarkPlan` carries `radius` and `shrink`, and the hit box, wind-up, burst and sparks read them; `TOLL` keeps radius 18 and takes the old `MARK.far` 10 as `shrink`, and `TOLL.gap` is gone.
- The fixed `HUD_ROOM = 120` test is replaced by the pure `hudPlace(stageTop, safeTop, hudHeight)` in `src/utils/hudPlace.ts`, with its gaps in `src/constants/hudPlace.ts`.
- `fitStage.ts` measures the HUD in its outside layout on every fit, and a `ResizeObserver` on `.hud` refits one frame later.

The developer made two small changes to the plan.
The burst's widening ring and glow now scale with the ring's radius, so phase 1 draws the same pixels as before and phase 2 bursts stay inside their own ring.
The pause button's bottom is taken as the larger of 8 px and the safe top, plus 30 px, which matches its CSS; the plan's safe top plus 8 plus 30 would have been stricter than the real layout on notched phones.

## Runtime dependencies

None were added.

## QA verdict

QA passed the patch on its first round.
Typecheck, lint and build pass, with only the chunk-size warning that was already there.
The smoke check passes for all nine heroes, 140 checks, with no page errors.
Oakheart reads 1,153 max HP at wave 5 in game, and the root line reads 16.
Wave 15 stills with Aldric in phase 2 show three separate rings that burst in order, and the art keeps the existing ramps, outlines, blink and glow.
A HUD sweep over 447 window sizes each for Brynn, Kael, Yun and Calder found no HUD above the window top, touching the pause button or over the stage while outside, and phones stay as they were.
HP lost in waves 1 to 4 matches the baseline for every hero on both bots, so only wave 5 changed.
Menus open with no errors, and a save from the base build loads with its coins and purchases.

QA listed four minor issues.
Ordinary runs still end at wave 5, though every failed run now leaves Oakheart lower.
In desktop windows such as 640 by 600, the HUD now sits inside the stage and covers roughly its top 40%, while 120 to 150 px of room above the stage stays empty.
The skilled bot stands idle above two Mire Toads in Brynn's wave 6 from wave 5, level 6 until the 30 minute timeout, which affects measurement only.
At 600 by 540 the HUD's place depends on the hero and the run, so Kael's HUD moves inside once when the build strip grows.
