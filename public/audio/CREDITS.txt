Warpblade sound credits

Every sound and music file in this folder comes from a free asset pack released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Credit is not required by the licence; it is given here as thanks.
The files were trimmed, made mono, loudness-matched and re-encoded as Ogg Opus for the game.
